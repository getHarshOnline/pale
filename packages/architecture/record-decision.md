# Prepare an architecture decision record

Identify the proposed change, who may decide it, observed constraints, date and conditions under which the choice applies. Preserve the alternatives, why each was rejected or deferred and what evidence would reopen the choice.

Prepare the record for the repository's existing decision home and name affected consumers. Separate the owner's accepted decision from a conditional proposal, the installed implementation and what consumers have actually tested. If a provider or dependency changes, identify which claims depend on it and what checks could confirm or reopen them. Label the record as proposed until its owner accepts it.

## Before handing off

- [ ] Record the question, choice and the reasons for it.
- [ ] Keep rejected alternatives and resulting constraints understandable.
- [ ] Name the condition that would reopen the decision.
