# Check the claims in an offer

Identify the offer, intended audience, supported promise, evidence and actual page or channel where it will appear. Give the marketing reviewer the product scope, available voice guidance, evidence against the proposal and permitted actions. Persuasive wording must stay within what the evidence supports.

In your reply, return a supported message or explain which claim still lacks evidence. Contacting people, spending, targeting and publishing remain with the owners who can authorise those actions.

## Before handing off

- [ ] Does the message address a named audience, task and real alternatives?
- [ ] Is each promised difference supported by evidence?
- [ ] Are unresolved claims clearly held out of the finished copy?
