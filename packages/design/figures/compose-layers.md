# Choose the relation between a live region and its page

## Choose the role before the library

Identify what this region contributes: atmosphere, explanation, pacing, orientation or an interaction with a particular object or relation. It need not pretend to be evidence to justify its presence. Preserve a selected source's role and intended fidelity. A shared identity treatment may legitimately recur; an allegedly page-specific explanation cannot substitute an unrelated reusable effect. Temporarily removing the region can reveal its contribution without removing facts or access paths. Use that experiment for diagnosis, not a quota to delete deliberate expression.

Choose how the region relates to its surroundings. It may change the ground beneath continuing content, insert a bounded figure, or deliberately cross a composition boundary. Scale, crop, edge and neighboring content should support that relation. A boxed effect is not wrong merely because it aligns to a grid; it is weak when the box substitutes for an intended relationship or reduces a required focal subject to an interchangeable accessory. Preserve logical order, readability and usable controls through a spatial departure. The local design owns its material names, crop permissions, expressive scale and boundaries.

Describe the meaningful behavior independently of its engine. A convergence, flow, subdividing contour or staged subject can retain its intended form across implementations. Those are examples, not a closed grammar all projects must register. Library defaults for color, antialiasing, exposure, blur or post-processing do not establish the intended look. Choose the existing drawing and timing mechanisms that serve the work; do not add another scene graph, animation engine or wrapper for appearances of integration.

## Pick how the region meets its surrounding reading field

A substrate changes the material under a passage. Keep labels and reading on a stable surface and check the most difficult composite. A plate presents an object with a clear edge, a reason for its scale and a natural caption or way out. A bleed carries a subject across a defined edge when that crossing does real compositional work. Keep readable controls, focus and the next task within reach. The same idea may use a contained plate on a narrow screen.

The polite-card failure is an expressive subject reduced to an interchangeable dark rectangle, a small caption and a default edge, without a selected relation to the surrounding page. Alignment or containment alone is not the defect: a modular figure among comparable peers can properly sit in a grid. If the intended effect changes the ground, compose it across that region; if it is an object, author its edge and scale; if it carries a passage across an edge, check both the local expressive allowance and the identity's crop rules. Those rules belong to the adopting design system, not a universal numbered scale.

Use the removal test above to identify the lost expression, atmosphere, orientation or interaction. A quieter material treatment may serve when the original adds nothing relevant; decorative purpose is not itself a reason to erase an approved treatment. Preserve a still that works before enhancement, with reduced motion and without a working graphics context. A page with no live region can still carry its identity through type, geometry, material and state cues at rest.

| Failure in the composition | Repair to compare |
| --- | --- |
| A ground change reads only as a generic bordered card | Change the material in place and keep the reading flow continuous. |
| An inserted figure has a default edge and incidental scale | Design its edge, crop and relation to the passage that refers to it. |
| A bleed is decorative overflow or covers controls | Restore the meaningful passage within its crop permission, or compose a plate. |
| Bleed text is rasterized or clipped at a narrow width | Keep meaningful text accessible and recompose its reading area. |
| Every subject receives the same box | Choose the relation per region; do not enforce a count of bleeds or effects. |
| A polished specimen exists only in a gallery | Inspect its actual consuming page before claiming that composition works. |

## Worked example

A current flows beneath a reading surface. Use a substrate if the flow changes the ground under the continuing text and the text stays legible. Use a plate if the figure is an object with a caption and one contained reading area. Let it bleed across a composition edge only when that passage expresses a relation, then clip it before it reaches neighboring controls. Remove the drawing briefly. If the reading still works and the pacing loses a meaningful transition, you have identified its actual job. If the page loses no information and no useful change in attention, reconsider the expense.
