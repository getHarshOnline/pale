# Compare expressive variants of an interface

Identify the page task, required information and controls, selected design direction, and the owner-approved range of expression. Put the intended emphasis in the existing brief and where the reader should encounter it. If the owner uses a scale, distinguish the required presence and permitted ceiling from the observed baseline and peak. Keep the selected attention pattern explicit. A coordinated comparison and a single focal moment can need different patterns. Use the owner's limits for this surface.

Compare the same content and task across the proposed treatments. Identify the key change, claim or object each treatment makes legible. Observe which region draws attention, what the reader can understand from it, and which state or action remains available. A palette swap or extra motion does not by itself change what the interface communicates. Test a deliberately flat or competing treatment when it would expose a weak criterion. Record missing emphasis, competition and loss of function as separate findings.

Treat theme, narrow layout and reduced motion as designed presentations. An emphasis carried by motion in one presentation may be carried by type, material, light or composition in another. Check the actual rendition, reading order, accessible names, visible and programmatic state, focus and the relevant action. Record the evidenced carrier and the intended state or meaning it preserves. A still image can support a composition observation. It cannot prove input behavior or the consequence of an action.

Check capability loss separately. With scripting or graphics unavailable, read and use the underlying document in the affected state. It should keep its content, meaningful order, reachable navigation, required evidence and held state. When expression competes with access or task completion, reduce the competing treatment before considering a change to content or behavior. Flag a change to the approved range or shared interaction contract for its owner.

## Preserve distinct identities without inventing differences

For related identities, start from the approved source and organizing idea of each. A page or shot consuming an accepted identity does not need a new brand derivation. When a new identity is actually requested, derive its material, geometry, type character, figure rhythm, crop, depth and temporal behavior from the subject and audience. Record meaningful choices in the existing project brief and their recurring values in the actual system. A fixed number of changed axes or a free recipe slot is not evidence of distinction.

Compare the complete treatment on equivalent content. Suppressing an accent or using neutral text can expose identities that differ only by branding labels. Use matched surfaces and states so content, framing or preference does not secretly supply the difference. Where useful, shuffle unlabelled captures for a reviewer who can explain the intended identities. Their explanation is a design judgment, not a numerical distinctness certificate. A grayscale filter changes the rendered composition; do not confuse it with an unchanged production view.

Use [the distinctness comparison](art-direction/compare-identities.md#make-the-distinctness-comparison-able-to-fail) when recognition is the claim: a reviewer who did not derive the treatments identifies shuffled stills, then records failing pairs by surface and state. Use [identity derivation](art-direction/derive-an-identity.md#carry-the-idea-into-the-system-that-renders-it) to connect the organizing idea to behavioral choices, token consumers and actual pages. Neither a token difference nor a generated stylesheet replaces that rendered comparison.

Include a quiet reading or reference surface as well as an expressive opening when the identity must serve both. Check relevant opened and generated surfaces through their actual entry and return path: search, settings, results, errors, navigation or an embedded artifact can reveal defaults hidden behind a branded landing. Follow the selected preference contract for explicit and system paths; an absent attribute may mean a local default rather than system preference. Identity disappearing in dark or reduced motion is a specific finding, not proof that every dimension needs a new value.

Retain useful shared construction and intentional neutral choices. A recurring geometry or crop can serve two genuinely different complete treatments. Repair a recoloring at the meaningful axes its source implies rather than amplifying accent or assigning artificial novelty to every primitive. Likewise, do not reduce a distinctive treatment to a palette to make component reuse easier. Its native owner determines the permitted variation. No shared token schema, fixed emitter set, mandatory world file, repository count or build-clean command is imposed here.

## Keep crossing ownership and behavior clear

When related identities link to or embed one another, resolve the actual source, target and relationship. The source owns its surrounding page; the referenced object retains its own identity and authoritative fields. A supplied subject, mention, membership or derivation relation is independent of whether its destination is internal or external. Do not infer a destination's authority or identity from link text, a hostname pattern or a shared engine. If a signature is unresolved, retain a useful link without inventing one.

Where the accepted design borrows a target signature, contain it within the reference and scale it to the reference's real role. An inline mention should not repaint a paragraph or become a card merely to show identity. An actual embedded artifact may need scoped target roles rather than the source theme's nearest color. Avoid a copied preset module that silently diverges from the native target. Payload and meaningful qualifications remain intact while its visual treatment changes.

Retain native navigation, modifier activation, focus and accessible purpose. Where a departure or relationship needs a cue, it must not depend only on color or disappear in forced colors. Keep any added accessible description useful and avoid repeating unhelpful destination labels on every ordinary link. The project decides its external-preview, new-context and federation-navigation policies; a compulsory family banner and a universal ban on one are both inappropriate defaults. A preview remains optional assistance rather than the only path to the destination.

If a new identity is authorized, follow it through the places that must use it: source brief, token/resolver registration, inherited components, consuming templates, cross-reference signature, supported preferences and affected entrypoints. Update only the lists and consumers that really exist. A generated stylesheet or a swatch in an atlas does not establish that pages and crossings use it. Keep native creation, publication, repository and account effects within their own authorization. This comparison grants none of them.

Put the comparison in the existing review or reply, identifying the builds and presentations observed, the emphasis and task outcome, the content and behavior preserved, limitations and decisions still open. The native owner supplies the scale, brand rules, components and browser route, and retains design approval.

Use [Preserve meaning across presentations](presentation.md) when the comparison reaches payload, role, relation or lifecycle interpretation. Ordinary browser observation does not require this expressive comparison; select it when the requested claim actually concerns variants or identity distinction.

## Before handing off

- [ ] Do access and behavior work across the presentations checked?
- [ ] Is each unresolved difference attached to the source or owner that can settle it?
