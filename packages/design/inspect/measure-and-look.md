# Measure the defect and read the whole treatment

## Measure and judge different questions

Read actual bounding boxes, winning declarations, rendered text and state. Check root overflow and clipped or fused text separately. Trace intended design values to token roles; a measured layout distance need not equal a token literal. Inspect title breaks, heading/paragraph rhythm, label-to-control proximity, title-to-metadata association, number/unit binding, source/qualification adjacency and visible pressed/focus states. Generate measurement labels from tool output, keeping reviewer commentary distinct. Retain parent captures beside any crops.

Check complete input and recovery paths using the selected journey method. An accessibility tree, automated scan and assistive-technology session have different coverage. Compare rendered payloads where presentation invariance is claimed: values, units, sources, qualifications, status and destinations must survive the transformation. A known-bad control or retained failing case should establish that a decisive check can detect its named fault. Do not rerun unchanged negative controls as ceremony.

Keep three conclusions separate: source/check soundness; observed browser behavior and appearance; and creative judgment against the originals and selected reference roles. Judge the whole treatment before relying on detector scores. A truthful, operable page can still be generic; an attractive capture can hide broken interaction. Missing evidence is blocked or not run, with its exact dependency. Neither a passing check nor a critic's clearance supplies owner acceptance or permission to publish.

Return findings tied to the exact route, condition, build and source cause, plus qualities that must survive the repair. Recheck failed cases and plausible regressions after correction. Broaden only when new evidence shows wider impact. Repeated nudging without closing a finding calls for a different diagnosis, not indefinite captures or weaker criteria.

Use [Design motion that helps people use the interface](../animate.md) when timing or interruption is in scope, and [Review the current design independently](../independent.md) when a separate reviewer is required. These are optional routes, not additional checklists for every capture.

Use [Compare expressive variants of an interface](../variations.md) when the requested claim concerns identity distinction or alternate treatments. An ordinary behavioral journey or local repair does not automatically require that review.

## Keep the measurements connected to the page

Read the build and browser's actual viewport, pixel ratio, preferences and platform. Start at the top, let fonts settle, traverse below the fold and check that delayed arrivals finished. If an image or drawing has not loaded, capture again or label what is unavailable. Preserve a whole-page image and the path to it when a crop is needed. A favorable crop can hide repetition, rhythm or the context for a control.

Review the full page in order. Look at the identity across material, geometry, type and light. Compare hierarchy, grouping and measure. Inspect whether the intended emphasis is visible without a wall of competing events. Then look at movement and feedback in time, and at whether values, units, sources and qualifications still read. Inspect title breaks, paragraph rhythm, spacing and focus at full scale where needed. A number on a review board comes from the measurement, with its element and conditions, rather than being copied from a desired token.

Use [Check the page before judging its treatment](sweep.md) for the ordinary failures on every rendered review pass, and [Judge the design through its working parts](lenses.md) for the design questions that follow.

## Worked example

A title and metadata appear fused on one site. Measure the title-to-metadata gap and compare the nearest unrelated gap. Check the delivered font and the content’s actual wraps. Read the whole opening against the design reference. A failing fixture can test a detector for fusion. Review readability and whether the composition suits the subject separately. Recheck the affected page and a neighbor after correcting the shared source.
