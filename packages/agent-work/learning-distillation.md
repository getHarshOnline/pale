# Find a reusable lesson in completed work

Use this guide when a completed task, investigation, defect, review or decision might improve a future occurrence. Return a proposal for the appropriate owner to review. Installing guidance or updating memory is a separate action.

Start with the original objective and context, observed signal, attempted approaches, decisive evidence, best-supported cause or distinction, intervention and verified outcome. Distinguish observation from explanation. Fluent discussion, one unexplained success, a self-reported improvement or a failure whose cause is disputed cannot establish a reusable rule.

Test where the lesson transfers. Name the future situations, observable triggers, required inputs, decision improved, rules that remain true, counterexample, unsafe contexts and dependencies on version, provider, jurisdiction, organisation or time. Generalise as far as the evidence supports. Keep project facts and owner preferences in their project or private home unless their transferable reasoning is independently established.

Search the existing guidance and choose the appropriate home:

- Add a decision rule or failure case to an existing method.
- Combine existing methods when their ordering is the reusable part.
- Propose a new method for an independently useful decision.
- Keep the lesson with its owner or environment when transfer depends on them.
- Discard a duplicate, trivial, unverified or too-narrow proposal.

Return the proposed lesson, applicability, evidence and confidence, decision rule, inputs, outputs, stopping conditions, negative cases, relationship to existing guidance, proposed owner, excluded local facts and checks needed before adoption. A later Package change needs permission from its maintainer, an originality review, validation through the actual system and a record of what changed.
