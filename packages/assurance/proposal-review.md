# Verify a proposed set of rules

Identify the exact candidate and available evidence, including superseded material and counterevidence. Check that every assertion preserves the source's scope, time, conditions, exceptions and units. Explain changes from the predecessor and identify who must settle disputed meaning. Reject the proposal or mark it unverified if an essential source, interpretation or effective-period boundary is missing.

Return a candidate whose claims can be traced to their sources, or a specific reason to stop. The verification result supports later owner review. It cannot attest, accept, activate, replace or silently update a Pack of rules.

## Before handing off

- [ ] Read the proposal against the applicable current source.
- [ ] Check assumptions, covered cases and missing qualifications.
- [ ] Leave sign-off with the person responsible for it.
