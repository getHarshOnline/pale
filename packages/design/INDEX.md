# Design

Package ID: `pale.design` · [Manifest](package.toml)

Carry meaning through design intent, shared roles and implementation into a rendered result. Develop brands, systems, interfaces, images and film through colour, type, layout, material and motion; inspect what the audience can perceive and do.

A workspace selects entries from this family for the duties its people and agents perform. To read it directly, open the page for your task and follow its reading links.
