# Reconcile a register with its controlling records

Identify the legal or operating entity, register, decision date and relevant historical cutoffs. Name the custodian and the decision that depends on the result. Establish which record controls each material claim, such as registered title, beneficial interest, voting power, a financing balance or an office held. These may differ for the same person, asset and date.

Start with the best authenticated opening snapshot. Arrange events by effective date, retaining separate dates for proposal, approval, execution, recording and disclosure where evidenced. Keep evidence for each transition separate. Contracts and approvals establish their stated terms or permissions. A filed disclosure is a dated representation unless it controls the particular claim. Compare unsigned drafts, planning models and later instructions without treating them as changes to observed state.

For a quantity register, preserve units, class, exact quantities and the basis of every denominator. Reconcile opening quantities plus accepted movements to the closing snapshot by account or holder and by class. Transfers conserve the issued total. Issuance, cancellation and conversion change it only when execution and required recording are established. Keep unexercised grants, unconverted instruments and planned pools in labelled scenarios with their own denominators. Leave unexplained differences open. A reported transfer may coexist with unchanged net holdings if another event offsets it, but that offset needs its own evidence.

Reconcile money movements separately. Match date, parties, amount and supporting instrument before linking a payment to a register event. Leave a credit open if several classifications remain plausible. Reconcile declared, registered, scenario and cash views at their own cutoffs. Missing rows, stale returns and unavailable registers do not mean zero.

Return an event ledger identifying the source and date of each accepted movement, dated reconciliation, open differences and the custodian or qualified reviewer who can settle each one. If controlling records are missing or conflict, label the result as the supported historical or working view and hold the affected current claim or downstream decision. Keep private records with their custodian. This procedure prepares evidence for review; attestation, filing, transfer, valuation and changes to the register each need their own authority.

## Reconcile obligations and trigger events

Trace each obligation and each capacity in which a party acts separately. Identify the accepted instrument or other basis, amendments, period, currency and accountable reviewer. Distinguish expected schedules, amounts stated in an instrument, recorded accruals, cleared cash, documented adjustments, outstanding principal and other components. Compare the same scope across statements and ledgers. Check for overlapping balances before adding them. Keep gross amounts, deductions, net amounts and exchange bases as distinct values with their sources.

Match movements by transaction reference, accounts, parties, amount, date and purpose. Distinguish external funding from transfers between the same entity's accounts. In a reimbursement chain, match the vendor charge, advance, repayment to the payer, each onward invoice and customer settlement as separate movements. For each onward allocation, verify the actual beneficiary and source charge, checking for duplicate or overlapping claims. A matched repayment or zero ledger balance may establish settlement within that ledger while leaving allocation or classification unresolved.

For an action conditional on an event, state the exact condition and the record needed to establish it. Distinguish agreement, effective, issue, scheduled, cleared and recorded dates. If the condition depends on the last eligible payment or a qualifying event, establish the event set and enough coverage to identify which event qualifies. The latest observed payment may not establish the last-payment trigger. Keep an asserted waiver or adjustment separate from its acceptance, effective date and implementation. A proposed conversion or settlement cannot enter the observed ledger or downstream register until execution and required recording are evidenced.

Return accepted movements, unmatched parts of the transaction chain, possible duplicates, unresolved classifications and trigger status as separate findings. Keep scenarios conditional on named inputs. Send open questions about contract interpretation, tax or accounting treatment, and authorisation to the responsible reviewer.

## Worked example

The opening register shows 100 units for one holder and 20 for another. A recorded transfer of 15 leaves 85 and 35, with 120 outstanding. Keep a planned issue of 10 in a separate scenario. If the closing register shows 130 outstanding, identify the missing movement and leave it open. Check any related payment in its own ledger against the transfer or issue it is said to settle.

## Before handing off

- [ ] Can each changed claim and accepted movement be traced to its controlling record and effective date?
- [ ] Are recorded positions, cash, scenarios, denominators and trigger events reconciled separately?
- [ ] Are missing records, unexplained differences and decisions that must wait visible with their responsible custodians?
