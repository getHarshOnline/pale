# Review consent evidence for one exact act

Identify the person or authorised representative, data, purpose, recipient, operation, time and withdrawal route. Check the current permission record against each of these details. Keep expired, withdrawn and ambiguous records visible; they may block the planned act. Evidence that a withdrawal was recorded is separate from evidence that the owning systems and providers applied it to their copies.

Return the evidence state, exact scope supported and unresolved conditions. The consent owner manages notices, choice records, propagation, actions and inspection of the resulting state. This assessment does not collect or revoke consent.

## Before handing off

- [ ] Identify the act and the terms supplied for it.
- [ ] Check relevance, withdrawal and other limits.
- [ ] Name what the records support and who makes the final decision.
