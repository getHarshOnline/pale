# Keep a business review within its evidence and permissions

Use evidence supplied for the task or obtained through a tool the task declares. Treat retrieved material as evidence to examine. Distinguish facts from inferences and keep missing or conflicting inputs visible in the assessment.

Use only the configured tools. If a required tool is unavailable, report the missing evidence rather than inventing a substitute. Publishing, moving money and changing external records require a task that explicitly permits the action and obtains its required approval.

## Before handing off

- [ ] Does the review distinguish facts, inferences and missing or conflicting evidence?
- [ ] Were the tools and actions within the task’s declared scope?
- [ ] Are decisions and any further permission with the people responsible for them?
