# Check what an engagement permits

## Keep each party's role clear

Start with the requested next step, its scope and the person responsible for deciding it. Read the engagement's own account and the relevant agreement or instruction. Keep four facts separate: who introduced the work, who contracted, who delivered and who was paid. Name the capacity in which each party acted. An introduction, reimbursement, repository membership or folder location answers only its own part of that account.

Carry the owner's settled decisions and permissions into the task. An outstanding commitment remains assigned when work pauses, unless the owner releases or changes it. Keep the work's lifecycle, delivery obligations, payment and permission for a particular action distinct. A paid invoice settles that payment; unfinished delivery remains unfinished.

## Continue the agreed work and identify a real change

Compare the requested step with the agreed responsibilities and limits. Continue work already authorised within that scope. Ask the responsible person only about a missing choice or a change that the existing agreement does not cover; explain the exact difference and why it matters. Do the independent preparation that is already permitted while that choice remains open.

Discovery, source access, testing, delivery and publication each have their own scope. An agreement to discuss work may leave access or delivery terms open. State what can be assessed from the available evidence and which observation needs further access. For a read-only review, keep customer contact, edits to client systems, spending, staffing commitments and publication within their separately granted permissions.

## Carry unfinished work through a pause or handoff

Read the latest owner instruction alongside the earlier agreement and the actual project state. Keep the remaining tasks with their responsible owners, including personal work or favours, and preserve why they are paused. A request to resume agreed work carries its existing scope and approvals forward. Reconcile changed access, counterparties or responsibilities where they affect the next action; ask once about any unresolved change.

Write the result on the engagement's owning page: the four roles, the settled commitments, what has been done, what remains and the evidence for any change. Put remaining work on its owner's list. Name a missing decision and its decision-maker beside the affected step so the rest of the authorised work can continue.

## Before handing off

- [ ] Are introduction, contracting, delivery and payment stated separately?
- [ ] Do the work list and handoff retain the owner's commitments and existing approvals?
- [ ] Does each open question name the actual change or missing choice and who can settle it?
