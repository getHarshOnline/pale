# Draft content for its reader and medium

Identify the audience, medium, reader question and communication objective. Use the owner-approved evidence and identified voice profile where relevant. Establish the main claim and keep unsupported material visible. Return the draft with source references, unresolved gaps and owner decisions. The owner determines where source knowledge lives, where writing belongs and which public route presents it.

For a long-form argument, use the article guide. For a new voice profile, use the voice guide. Find both in the family index for direct reading or the generated reference index in Pale. For variants, apply cross-medium adaptation to the same supported claims. The draft is a candidate for the required review and checks.

## Before handing off

- [ ] Does the draft serve its reader and intended action?
- [ ] Do sources, rights and open questions remain with the relevant claims?
- [ ] Has the draft been read, with remaining review identified?
