# Review the contents of a release

Start with one immutable candidate and its intended recipient or use. Identify the inherited source revisions, build and delivery routes under review. Follow the release-materials method and name who can settle rights, notices and permission to release.

In the review reply, compare the actual contents with their declarations and source-delivery obligations. Return the inventory and gaps even if a decision remains pending, assigning each unresolved rights or delivery question to its owner.

## Before handing off

- [ ] Compare the release contents with its declarations.
- [ ] Check inherited notices and source delivery for included material.
- [ ] Leave any unresolved issue with its owner.
