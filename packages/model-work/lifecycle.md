# Review a model through its lifecycle

Identify the data, run, evaluation, artifact, serving, monitoring and rollback evidence by their exact versions. Apply the lifecycle guide and independent machine-learning review to the candidate, baseline and deployment requirements.

In the review, report findings for each transition, the first unsupported step, its consequence and the observation that could settle it. Keep invalid controls, missing critical subgroups, uncertainty and recovery needs visible. Recommend whether to promote using the supported evidence; the accountable owner decides. Starting jobs, deploying or promoting are separate actions.

## Before handing off

- [ ] Are the data, model and versions under review identifiable?
- [ ] Have evaluation, promotion and rollback requirements been checked?
- [ ] Is it clear what supports adoption and who decides?
