# Research a market choice

Read the decision, owner, time horizon, threshold, supplied evidence and constraints. Judge source quality, then define the market, distinguish demand and supply, reconcile independently reasoned estimates and test rival explanations. Retrieve current public evidence through a permitted tool. If access is missing, keep the gap visible rather than inventing a market size.

In the analysis, return the market definition, equations and ranges, sources and assumptions, conflicting evidence, sensitivity and switching conditions, segments that change the action, and a scoped recommendation. Identify dated facts needing refresh. Stop when the choice holds across plausible values or a critical unknown has been isolated for measurement.

Customer contact, commissioned research, data purchases, publication and changes to a commercial plan each need the appropriate tool and approval beyond this analysis.

## Before handing off

- [ ] Are the decision, alternatives and period clear?
- [ ] Are observed facts, evaluation criteria and interpretation distinct?
- [ ] Is it clear how the result could change the choice?
