# Keep data, models and decoration legible

## Keep semantic content and imagery honest

Bind the region to the source, actual data and state. A decorative field must not imply measured signal through invented quantities, axes, counters, timestamps or instrument labels. An explanatory diagram can illustrate a supplied mechanism without inventing a sample size or computed outcome. A simulation or calculator needs its actual supplied model, assumptions, units and appropriate qualification; the fact that it runs does not validate its science or predict a real outcome.

For informative output, retain the necessary text, values, qualifications and destinations through an appropriate accessible representation. A browser graphic must not become the sole path to a substantive fact or action. If the native publication derives the enhancement from rendered markup, preserve that input contract. Another application may properly use a typed model or authorized API. Keep the facts in one maintained source. Do not expose secrets or private machine state in DOM attributes merely to make inspection easier. Inspect semantic state and implementation machinery through their proper tools.

Keep an informative text alternative available when the enhanced drawing mounts. Replacing a fallback image must not also remove its only description. Decorative graphics need no unnecessary focus or announcement; a meaningful interactive object needs the appropriate name, state and input paths. A staged volume's thickness or position should not imply length or rank the source never supplied. Keep its actual identity, collection order and destination available. Generated imagery is not documentary evidence of a real object merely because it looks plausible.

## Choose the subject and what changes on screen

A region can give atmosphere, explain a relationship, pace a passage or answer input. State its primary job. For an explanation, keep the sourced values, units, qualifications and an accessible table, list or description outside the drawing. For atmosphere, avoid axes, timestamps, instrument labels or plausible readings that could look like a measurement. A visualization cannot invent an estimate unless the project supplies and approves its computation and meaning.

| If the idea is | A possible construction | What to verify |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A continuous quantity or material | Scalar field, gradient or isoline | The value or behavior matches its source. An ambient field carries no measurement labels. |
| Transport or direction | Vector field, advection or a flow path | Direction or rate follows the explanation. The still conveys the important relationship. |
| Periodic structures meeting | Interference, summed waves or overlapping rasters | Marks do not imply an unsupported reading or scale. |
| Ordered layers or levels | Sedimentation, registration, recursive contours or enumeration | Order, identity and any source labels survive as steps or a composed still. |
| A set resolving into a relation | Discrete points, a neighbor graph or structured convergence | The points and their relation stay recognizable across input and release. |
| A structure emerging from randomness | Signal and noise, filtering or thresholded noise | Decorative texture is not presented as a measured signal. |
| A known surface displaced | Domain warping or a lens/refraction map | The deformation serves the selected subject and keeps its important features recognizable. |
| A material changing in place | Masks or a dissolve between surfaces | The reading plane and state remain clear through the change. |
| Depth and focus | Layered planes or a depth-of-field treatment on a still | Apparent depth does not obscure content or invent a rank or quantity. |

Name the drawing behavior before choosing its engine. Geometry, composition, typography, material and motion can distinguish two uses of the same behavior. A library's antialiasing, lighting and post-processing defaults need their own check. The page owner supplies which effects fit its setting, and a still keeps the same useful composition when a device cannot draw the live version.

Keep the behavior specific. Diffusion spreads from a source; oscillation returns about a rest; recursive subdivision creates finer cells; registration aligns layers; latching settles into a detent; drape forms folds under an authored or computed weight. Sedimentation builds ordered layers, while enumeration advances a labeled sequence without implying that layers stack or age. These are useful constructions, not a closed list imposed on every project. If the adopting system registers candidate behaviors, its owner supplies that vocabulary and its permitted use. Sharing a construction does not prove shared identity, and changing only its name does not prove a different treatment.

## Worked example

A chart’s flowing lines depict measured rates. Keep the units, period and values in the same data source as the figure and provide a readable table. If a different field is atmosphere, leave off axes and counters that would make it look measured. A staged book can show a real title and link without making its invented thickness an assertion about page count. Keep the caption when replacing the static image with the enhanced drawing.
