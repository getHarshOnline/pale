# Watch the delivered film and inspect difficult joins

## Judge the delivered sequence

Watch the actual encode at intended speed and with its selected sound. Inspect joins, intermediate masks, clipped or doubled glyphs, the complete reading window and ending closely. Compare decoded frames with native snapshots when diagnosing capture defects; a successful encode can contain clipped geometry. A contact sheet, renderer pass and self-reported complete review each establish less than an observed connected playback.

Review against the original requested qualities and retained reference roles, not only the builder's narrowed specification. Keep required creative gaps open even if labeled polish or a limitation. A focused revision can reuse unchanged evidence, while reopening affected joins, timing and findings. Independent review follows the selected duty; `review-film` in the Design manifest supplies the formal story and delivery review when required. Publication, owner acceptance and technical delivery remain separate.

## Review the film the viewer will actually receive

Check the editable composition and the delivered encode separately. A clean render command can produce a file with missing text, a fallback font, different timing or a mistaken audio track. Open the encoded media at its planned display size. Check the start, last frame, joins, holds, color, audio or intended silence, caption coverage and player behavior. Compare requirements from the brief and original references with their specific intervals. An exact match to one shot does not establish a requirement about the whole sequence.

For review, give an independent reader the original request, the applicable reference observations and the prepared plan or encoded result. A source ID connects them to the intended material. The reader still has to decide whether the motion, staging and result carry the requested meaning. Keep actual frame or playback observations separate from approvals, and test a derived file again when an edit, encode or overlay could change the observation. Check the visible and accessible delivery, including a meaningful description or transcript when the placement needs it.

Revisit the chosen treatment and the simpler alternative from the shot plan where added machinery was a consequential decision. In the actual encode, does the depth, camera or transformation make the relationship easier to understand? Count the reads within each beat again, including text inside captured interfaces, and check them at the smallest intended fitted picture. A legible title does not compensate for an unreadable screenshot or a result that disappears before it registers.

## Worked example

Play the exported film with sound at its intended rate. Scrub across a transition with a mask, examine the decoded intermediate frames, and compare the corresponding scene view. If the encode clips a glyph or joins too early, reopen the shot and its neighbors. Time the legible interval for the full phrase instead of approving the title from the first clean still.
