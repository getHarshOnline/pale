# Keep light and state when the effect changes

## Keep access and state when the carrier changes

Provide the supported reduced-transparency or unsupported-filter presentation without losing the surface's role or access to its controls. A more opaque fill, edge or tone distinction can carry depth. Respond to actual preference and capability conditions rather than inferring them from viewport size. The native owner supplies detection, stored choices and precedence.

Inspect meaningful state without its glow or texture. In [forced colors](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Reference/At-rules/@media/forced-colors), box and text shadows can be suppressed and colors substituted. A marker carried only by a shadow can disappear; retain an appropriate text, outline, border or other semantic/visual carrier. Make targeted adjustments where the result needs them. Do not disable the user's palette merely to preserve the original aesthetic.

Reduced motion should retain state and intended emphasis through an appropriate in-place treatment. Check both acquiring and releasing the state. A blanket animation override must not strand a hidden surface or prevent cleanup. The motion method owns clock, interruption and timing judgment; this resource owns what material and light communicate. Not every entrance needs scale, blur and opacity together, and not every reduced result needs another animated decay.

Exercise the affected surface in its real composition: light/dark where supported, the relevant preferences, changed background and held/released state. Use actual contrast and performance observations for those claims, then judge whether the material reads at ordinary viewing scale. A magnified specimen, swatch atlas or generated token file does not prove application to a page or film. Keep observed behavior, creative judgment and owner acceptance separate.

## Worked example

A selected row has a small steady core with a falloff. In reduced motion, change the marker in place. In forced colors the shadow can disappear, so keep an outline or a meaningful label and the programmatic state. In reduced transparency, make the floating layer opaque enough to keep its role. Toggle each setting while the row is held and verify release, focus and the resting result.
