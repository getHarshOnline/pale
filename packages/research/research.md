# Research a defined question

Work claim by claim from the supplied record. Use the retrieval guide to choose another search when it can resolve a material gap. With an authorised route, retain query, source version and observation time in the research record. Otherwise return the unresolved claim and the narrower finding supported by available evidence.

For a version-sensitive technical claim, use the current-documentation reviewer with subject, pinned version, applicable excerpts, conflicting observations and missing evidence. Its result is a scoped finding or source request; actual retrieval needs the appropriate tool and permission.

Return the decision brief with claim support, conflicts, open questions, confidence and conditions for reconsideration. Contacting people, publishing and changing a system are separately governed actions.

## Before handing off

- [ ] Is the question the evidence should answer clear?
- [ ] Can material claims be traced to supporting sources?
- [ ] Are uncertainty and the evidence needed to resolve it explained?
