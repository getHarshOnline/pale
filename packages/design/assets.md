# Make an asset that belongs in its destination

Use this for a still, illustration, material, model or coordinated set. Study the accepted references and decide what the asset must keep, what may vary, and what the reader should get from it. The project owns its subjects, rights, canon and final choices.

| If you are doing this | Read this |
| --- | --- |
| Choose a still or lock a treatment | [Choose the treatment before making a set](assets/choose-a-treatment.md) |
| Extend, repair or adapt a source | [Preserve what the supplied asset already gets right](assets/preserve-a-source.md) |
| Export, encode and inspect the variants | [Check the required asset variants where they will appear](assets/check-the-export.md) |
| Carry a treatment across a set of assets | [Keep an approved treatment coherent across a set](assets/keep-a-set-coherent.md) |
| Model, export or reduce geometry | [Prepare a model for its actual renderer](assets/prepare-a-model.md) |
| Turn a keyframe or prepared source into generated footage | [Make and check a generated shot](assets/check-generated-motion.md) |
| Compose a plate, repair one region or check a keyframe | [Make and check a still or keyframe](assets/make-and-check-a-still.md) |

## Before handing off

- [ ] Does each variant carry the intended subject and reference qualities?
- [ ] Are the source’s settled qualities preserved?
- [ ] Does the exported asset work in its actual placement?
- [ ] Are audience files clear of private source and production details?
