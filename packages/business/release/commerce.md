# Review a shop's launch rehearsal

Identify the seller, storefront version, commerce account, environment, selected product variants and intended customers. For each handoff, name its owner and where to read back the result. Keep the catalogue, sellable stock, order, payment, fulfilment, notification, return and refund states separate. Read the applicable support requirements and policies from their owners.

Compare what the storefront shows and sends to checkout with the product variant, price and availability in the system that owns them. Review an authorised order rehearsal through fulfilment and any promised return and refund. Review a separate cancellation before fulfilment. Match each outcome to its originating order and read back the payment, stock and fulfilment consequences separately. A return can be recorded before its refund settles; cancellation may need a different way to undo its effects.

Include a stale storefront variant and an apparent success with a missing downstream change in the rehearsal. Check whether the evidence detects both. A customer confirmation leaves missing, delayed or mismatched downstream results unresolved. Explain what a fixture exercised separately from what ran in the actual launch environment, and name who can settle each open question.

In the review, give the evidence for each customer journey and responsible owner, remaining launch blockers, and which sales and availability claims are supported. This task reviews evidence. Creating orders, moving money, publishing claims and approving launch require their own permissions.

## Before handing off

- [ ] Has the intended storefront's purchase path been inspected?
- [ ] Do the product, terms and transaction checks cover the planned launch?
- [ ] Are unresolved obligations and the go-live decision with their owners?
