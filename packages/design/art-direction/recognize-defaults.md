# Recognize unconsidered design choices

Use this catalogue when a plan or rendered design feels generic, timid, excessive or unlike the chosen direction. A familiar technique is a lead to investigate. Establish what it does for this subject, reader and reference role before calling it a failure. A count of tells cannot decide the quality of a design.

Name the page this category usually produces and its predictable opposite. If the proposal is one of those without a reason from the brief, changing the palette will not resolve it. Compare the opening, order of information, main object and interaction. Shared controls and useful templates can serve different subjects well; approved reconstruction can deliberately resemble its source.

## Give the finding a direction

| Failure | What the correction should do |
| --- | --- |
| Intended presence is missing | Restore the requested quality at the level where it is defined. A request for more character cannot be answered by another restraint rule. |
| Expression competes with the task | Reduce the competing emphasis before deleting useful information. |
| The wrong device carries the idea | Change the means of expression. Making decorative apparatus quieter does not make it a working instrument. |

In the existing review, name the location and state, what is visible, what the reader loses, the reason a technique is unearned, the likely correction and what must survive it. An untested explanation remains a hypothesis. Where the content earns a default, say how: a genuine sequence earns numbers, an actual comparison may earn equal columns, and a chosen paper material can earn a warm palette. The following checks help make that judgment concrete.

## Look for clusters of defaults

| Tell | What to inspect and what could justify it |
| --- | --- |
| Centred headline, sub-line and button | Does the opening reveal the subject, or merely announce a category? Compare a composition led by its characteristic object. A centred composition can suit the actual content. |
| Three equal icon-heading-text cards | Check whether the three things have equal weight and the same kind of information. Let scale and anatomy follow their differences. |
| Bento feature tiles | Mixed box sizes can still hide one interchangeable feature list. Give distinct information distinct structure; use tiles when their relationships earn them. |
| Checkmarks or icons on every list item | A mark should communicate a real state or distinction. Otherwise a list can remain a list. |
| Decorative two-colour gradient | Identify its material, light source, state or illustrative purpose. A wash alone does not establish atmosphere or identity. |
| Tracked uppercase label above every block | Keep the label when it adds a classification the heading cannot. Choose case as part of the type system; inspect the render rather than declaring every capital a defect. |
| Word, dash and fragment headings | A heading such as “Precision — measured twice” can obscure what the section contains. Prefer the thing's name or a meaningful labelled field. |
| Middle-dot metadata chains | Repeated `a · b · c` can flatten different relationships. Compare labelled fields, columns or a short sentence. |
| Arrows on every link, including sliding hover arrows | Keep arrows for an actual direction or relationship. The control's form and verb can identify an action without another animated mark. |
| Emoji substituted for interface icons | Check the intended icon language, accessible name and words expressing status. A decorative emoji must not become the only control label. |
| One stock line-icon set and automation sparkles | Check whether marks belong to the design's geometry and whether the feature is intelligible in words. Reusing a suitable icon is different from letting a stock set supply the identity. |
| `01 / 02 / 03` on unrelated sections | Number an order the reader needs. A decorative sequence must not imply a process or ranking that the content lacks. |
| Every section fades and slides upward | Ask which arrival explains a change or earns attention. Continuous entrances can hide the intended emphasis; static content can remain immediately available. |
| Every card lifts, scales or gains shadow on hover | First establish whether the card is a control. An actionable card needs coherent focus and pressed states; a container does not need to pretend to be clickable. |
| Warm paper, serif and clay accent | Check that the material and voice come from this brief rather than the category “thoughtful editorial.” |
| Near-black with an acid or purple accent | Establish the use scene and material. “Technical” or “bold” alone does not select a dark palette. |
| Rainbow decoration, stock pastels or neon | Spend chroma where attention or meaning needs it. Check roles and contrast instead of applying a shelf palette. |
| Untinted white ground | Check whether the ground was chosen for the content and medium. A paper world can have a designed temperature; white is not wrong merely because it is white. |
| Tinted near-black as the automatic dark ground | Design the dark ground from material and use, instead of choosing a colour just off black by habit. |
| Dark as inverted light | Inspect ground, ink, key light, shadows and emission independently. Swapping foreground and background can erase depth or overwhelm the same accent. |
| Reduced motion as a dead page | Preserve each meaningful state, relation and intended expressive quality through an appropriate reduced form. Do not freeze a sequence mid-frame or remove its conclusion. |
| Mobile as stacked desktop | Compose the opening, reading order and next action for the smaller view and its input. Source-order stacking can bury the central relation. |
| Mobile as desktop with pieces missing | Explain each context-dependent omission and retain access to necessary information and destinations. Space pressure alone does not justify removing a task. |
| One interface sans for display, reading and system text | Check what each role needs. Inter, Geist or Space Grotesk are examples to question when chosen automatically, not prohibited fonts. |
| Monospace as a technical costume | Commands, identifiers, code and aligned figures have concrete needs. Reading and display need their own decisions; a genre label does not settle them. |
| Every container has one soft radius and grey shadow | Give elevation and edges a role. Much content can be grouped without a box. |
| Huge opening number with a small qualification | Verify the figure's role and keep its unit, period and limitation attached. A number should lead because the subject requires it. |
| One ruled ledger for evidence, offers, narrative and projects | Compare the questions those forms answer. Shared components need not force unrelated information into identical rows. |
| One accent-coloured, italic or bold word per headline | Check whether the emphasis expresses a distinction or is an automatic decoration. Let the chosen type role carry the headline when no extra distinction is needed. |
| Hairlines, edition labels and dense columns as instant editorial identity | Rules can compare values and establish useful groups. A broadsheet costume does not by itself explain the subject. |
| Hero, features, proof, pricing and call to action on every page | Write the reader's actual sequence of questions. Keep a template when that sequence fits, rather than manufacturing content for its slots. |
| Three pricing columns with the middle one raised and badged | Compare the real scopes, prices and decision. Do not imply a preferred offer or invent tiers to fill the layout. |
| Illustration presented as operational proof | A mock screen can explain a concept; it cannot establish a working capability. Keep its illustrative role clear and use actual evidence for factual claims. |
| A blank wait or lone spinner followed by a layout jump | Show useful pending state and reserve the expected space. Choose a skeleton only when its shape helps explain what will arrive; do not invent progress. |
| Missing privacy or terms route where the operation needs one | Establish the actual data handling, offer and responsible publisher. Reach the approved workspace-held legal text rather than generating legal authority from the design. |
| Generic or evasive interface copy | Replace “Submit” with the actual action where helpful; errors explain the state and remedy. Question automatic apologies, sales language in explanation, repeated “not X, but Y” reversals and dash-joined headlines. |

A broken state, inaccessible destination, misleading capability claim or missing required legal route does not need a cluster of aesthetic tells to matter. Conversely, several familiar forms can be justified by their actual jobs. Compare the rendered result with the requested outcome.

## Diagnose the whole treatment

### Empty polish and the polite card

Large neutral ground, thin type and generous gaps can substitute for a decision. Check whether space helps reading, separation or a deliberate pause; padding that only extends the scroll does not. A bounded, intentionally sparse statement can earn silence.

An intended expressive figure can also disappear inside a bordered card the same size as its neighbours. Remove it temporarily: if nothing in the composition or understanding changes, identify the quality or relation it was meant to carry. Its useful form might sit in the page's material, occupy a distinct plate or cross a grouping. The answer depends on its role, not a requirement that every figure bleed beyond a column.

### Costume instead of capability

Prompt glyphs at rest, blinking cursors, scanlines, phosphor prose, fake session logs, stamps, graph paper and gauges can imitate an instrument while measuring nothing. An addressable, searchable interface with explicit state can carry the intended directness without those props. A real query field or a work about the period can justify them.

Neon, cyan-magenta pairs, glitch, HUD brackets and data rain can similarly name a genre instead of expressing the subject. A degraded signal or a meaningful crossing can earn an effect; a narrative explicitly set in that genre can use its material. Ask what the effect tells the reader.

### Containers and atmosphere become the subject

Watch for bands, rails, steppers, coloured side stripes, nested cards and rules above and below every row whose only job is to announce a section. Compare spacing, alignment and type first. Keep a container when its edges identify an object the reader acts on or carries away, such as a form, record or figure.

Blurred orbs, radial washes and tiled grain behind all content can soften the edges where values must be read. Keep texture and light tied to material, state or a bounded atmospheric scene; preserve a clear reading plane. Haze may be the content of a narrative region. It is not a substitute for deciding every page's light and depth.

### Recolouring and one repeated trick

When different identities must be recognisable, suppress the accent and compare geometry, type, material, crop, light and rhythm. Colour can contribute; colour alone does not establish the requested distinction. Shared neutral controls need not become different merely to pass this experiment.

A texture, cursor or shape stamped everywhere can exhaust its meaning after one page. A single device can still carry a coherent identity when it is developed across the relevant choices and varies with context. Keep the coordinated idea, rather than multiplying tricks or giving the same device new names.

### Spectacle and cleverness without a job

Glow, parallax, scale and motion spend attention. Identify the meaning behind their strongest moments and whether the selected rhythm leaves room to read, compare and recover. Do not impose one universal peak count: an ambient work, a coordinated comparison and a narrative may need different rhythms.

A wordplay headline, novel control or unusual layout should make something clearer or express a quality the plain form cannot. If the novelty only makes the reader decode style first, compare the plain form. Poster-sized type, off-grid crops and dense interruptions can suit a narrative opening and still be wrong for sustained reference or decision work.

### Restraint answers a request for presence

Compare the actual before and after. A display face called too familiar must not become an undesigned platform default; a request to stop churn must not strip the settled expression. When the response to “almost invisible” is another prohibition, the repair may already be moving the wrong way. Restore the missing quality within the selected design and check that it is visible on the real page.

A specimen or specification proves neither adoption nor visibility. Trace where the material, light or motif reaches actual pages. A texture beneath an almost opaque wash is present in source and absent to the reader. Apply the decision where it belongs rather than writing another description of it.

### An addition remains separate from the work

Set a new section or correction beside the existing page. Different naming systems, conflicting status labels or a correction pasted above unchanged content can expose an unfinished integration. Revise the affected composition into one coherent result. Deliberate contrast can have a job; accidental differences between revisions do not.

### Apparatus, facts and internal commentary

A pulsing live dot needs live state. An inert prompt must not look usable. Build numbers, plate numbers, clocks, weather strips and “just now” timestamps need the relationship or observation they claim. Remove the false implication, connect the display to real state, or explain its illustrative role. Fictional apparatus and authored UI remain valid in their declared context.

Never invent sample sizes, intervals, measurements, case outcomes, APIs, quotations or testimonials to fill a design. Irregular-looking numbers are still invented numbers. Keep supplied values and units together; use an honest gap, title-only record or labelled placeholder where needed. Do not turn a decorative curve into a claimed computation.

Keep incidental production commentary and machinery out of the reader's way. Preserve attribution, edition context, uncertainty, methodology and illustration labels when they help the reader interpret the subject truthfully. An engine or design system can be the actual subject of an article or manual; that differs from putting its branding or token dashboard into unrelated publications.

### Trends and one early reference decide everything

A trend list or animated component collection can supply useful mechanics without choosing the page's identity. Derive values from the existing design roles and actual brief. A copied palette, duration, scale or font stack still needs that decision. Passing a craft checklist does not establish that the whole treatment fits the subject.

For example, a refracting surface can suit an optical material. It still needs legible content, an opaque reduced-transparency form and a complete fallback where the filter is unsupported. “Liquid glass is current” does not answer those questions.

If every proposal repeats one early image's object, crop or composition, return to the gathered references and compare alternatives on the same content. Select the qualities the board supplies without assuming its first image dictates the answer. For inspiration, seek the intended family resemblance. For reconstruction or licensed source reuse, preserve the requested fidelity and permitted adaptations; resemblance is then evidence of success, not a reason to redesign it.

## Judge a cinematic treatment in time

A sequence should make a temporal relation more intelligible or vivid. Ordering feature labels over unrelated images does not establish that relation. Compare the opening, central change and payoff, with explanatory copy removed briefly where that helps reveal what the images carry.

Check for uninterrupted intensity, a film repeated across pages with unrelated purposes, unintended identity drift, garbled lettering, preset transitions and the same slow push on every still. A designed UI or material morph with clear correspondences is different from accidental drift. A meaningful expressive opportunity can also be lost by reducing everything to interchangeable diagrams.

For a page sequence, preserve the reader's control and a route past it. Capturing or slowing scroll, adding a loading gate or hiding content behind playback needs scrutiny against the chosen host contract. Autoplay and an intentional hold are not defects merely by existing. Judge time against the selected playback or interaction model. Inspect dark, reduced and mobile forms where that page supports them; those are not automatic requirements for every standalone film.

Use the native film review for full playback, transitions and applicable sound. A still cannot establish timing. Keep a justified cinematic form when it advances the subject and serves its host, rather than responding to an empty spectacle by removing all temporal expression.

## Worked comparisons

An inspection page has working search, honest state and keyboard access, but its identity exists only in a specimen. Keep the behaviour. Trace the missing type, material and light into the real template; do not replace the search or add more promises about the design.

A figure recomputes from supplied observations and helps explain a claim. Its graticule is part of the instrument; an unrelated stamped frame is a prop. Remove the frame for a comparison, keeping the values, units and interpretation. Restore any edge whose removal actually loses grouping.

A practice explains a chain of work through one recurring object changing over time. The sequence can earn its form when each transformation makes that chain understandable. Applying the same film to every service page merely because it looked good would need a separate justification for each page's question.
