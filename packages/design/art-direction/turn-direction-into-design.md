# Turn direction into visible decisions

## Translate direction into constraints

For a new direction, put the decisions needed for implementation in the existing brief or specification. For a focused repair, apply the relevant settled decisions and revisit only those the change reaches. Use these dimensions as applicable:

- hierarchy: what dominates, what recedes, and how users scan dense and sparse states;
- typography: voice, reading role, scale relationships, and fallback behavior;
- color and material: semantic roles, contrast obligations, and where emphasis is deliberately scarce;
- geometry: spacing rhythm, alignment, corner and border logic, and responsive reflow;
- imagery and iconography: subject, treatment, provenance, consistency, and empty-state behavior;
- motion: the state change it explains, reduced-motion alternative, interruption, and completion state; and
- interaction: focus, hover, pressed, selected, loading, error, success, and disabled behavior.

Reuse existing tokens and components when they can express the direction. Introduce a new token or primitive only when it names a repeated semantic decision. A one-off visual exception is not automatically a system.

Plan attention across the visit as well as within one frame. Place emphasis where the subject's meaning concentrates, and design the opening and ending deliberately. Distinguish the owner's required presence from its permitted ceiling. A single focal peak, coordinated comparison and ambient composition can have different legitimate rhythms; apply the selected pattern rather than a universal peak count. Mobile use may require a different staging of the same information and next action. Keep reader-needed source order and access.

When feedback asks for more character, change the relevant identity axis instead of defaulting or removing it. When it asks for a quality without a device, preserve the quality through a different construction. A quieter treatment reduces competing expression before deleting information or flattening all hierarchy. A request to stop churn preserves settled meaning and contracts; it does not freeze prototype pixels. In an implementation pass, identify what changed visibly and whether still-open feedback survived.

## Worked example

A technical account needs both precision and atmosphere. Keep the values, labels and body legible on a quiet reading plane. Give the opening figure one clearly motivated material effect. Compare a reduced treatment and check whether the atmosphere survives with the effect removed. Keep the treatment that serves this subject.
