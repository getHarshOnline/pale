# Read the reference for the qualities it supplies

## Study a reference for its selected role

Classify the intended transfer before extracting qualities:

| Role | Study and carry forward |
| --- | --- |
| Inspiration | The quality or stance it contributes to this brief; derive a treatment rather than silently copying its identity. |
| Selected reconstruction | The actual composition, typography, mechanisms and timing at the requested fidelity, with deliberate adaptations. |
| Approved reuse | The selected source or asset, its rights, dependencies and permitted modification or hosted use. |

Keep the source and relevant passage, state or time range available beside the selected quality, fidelity and authority. Use the existing brief or board; do not create a second reference register. The consuming plan accounts for retained, adapted and unselected roles with their reasons and affected regions. A required quality stays open until delivered or superseded by applicable authority. Selection for inspiration grants neither asset reuse nor approval of the final output.

For reconstruction, read the original prompt, implementation and assets together, then traverse the running original through its opening, controls, transitions and ending. Preserve the selected mechanism before refining assets. Compare original and work in matched states. A missing runnable original limits the comparison; source inspection does not become visual proof. Keep a source outcome distinct from a proposed device so review can catch both omission of a requested quality and addition of an unsupported constraint.

For an open direction, gather and annotate decision-bearing references before letting an early generated image fix the entire treatment. A board should communicate a coherent center and range, using the owner's vocabulary and explicit exclusions. Keep useful alternatives distinct while the choice is open; converge within delegated authority rather than requesting approval again for a settled choice. A hosted style code is a provider-specific derivative, not a replacement for the source board or its rights.

Explore alternatives on meaningful axes or from different subject references. More intense versions of one idea are not divergent directions. Compare equivalent content at the target scale; suppress a dominant accent when it would conceal identical geometry, type and material. Merge named useful qualities into one coherent treatment instead of averaging the candidates. Keep only rejected alternatives whose reasons help future decisions.

## Separate what the reference supplies from what it happens to contain

An inspiration can supply a quality such as the cut of a shape or the continuity of a transition. A reconstruction can require a particular state or relationship to survive. Reuse can start from the actual source. Record the role at the passage, state or example that matters. A reference for light need not set typography, and a new brief need not reopen an agreed reference for another part of the work.

For an open direction, compare a few independent proposals on what they change in material, geometry, type, light, space or time. Include one simple treatment that answers the task. Discuss where each holds and where it fails. Translate feedback into a specific consequence for the reader or treatment, and keep the original words separately when that translation is open to dispute.

## Worked example

A selected reference uses stable typography above a scrubbed cut. Watch the whole sequence, then inspect the holds and compare the starting and final frames. Preserve the real reading plane and clock when the brief calls for reconstruction. If the same source is only inspiration for the new work, carry the useful quality and check how it reads with the new subject.
