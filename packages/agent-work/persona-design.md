# Develop and test an agent persona

Establish the intended relationship, interaction qualities and governing limits, then use the persona guide. In the proposed specification, settle the central identity before developing its voice, defining tension, behavioural limits, name, safe identifier and visual direction. Test correction, refusal, ambiguity, urgency and repeated use; identify contradictions to revise. Rendering, acceptance into a Pack or configuration, file creation, installation and activation are separate steps for their owners.

## Before handing off

- [ ] Is it clear what the adviser helps its reader decide?
- [ ] Are its role limits and responses to missing or conflicting inputs clear?
- [ ] Have representative cases established that the persona is ready?
