# Review the literature for a scholarly question

Identify the question, review protocol, sources, dates, access limits and intended decision. Judge source roles and independence, then use the literature guide to search, screen, appraise, synthesize and check citations. Deliver the protocol, evidence record, study synthesis, contradictions, uncertainty and update trigger in the review. Leave a conclusion open when material access is missing or support is insufficient. Use the owning tools and permissions for retrieval and saving artifacts.

## Before handing off

- [ ] Does the question define search and inclusion scope?
- [ ] Were sources read before claims were attributed to them?
- [ ] Are strength, disagreement and missing coverage visible?
