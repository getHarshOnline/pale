# Test whether an established method already covers the claim

Use this guide when a literature review must decide whether a proposed mechanism, construct or representation contributes something distinct. Define the claim, domain, assumptions, outcome or property to preserve, and the decision the comparison could change. Formal expressibility, empirical adequacy, usability and research value can yield different findings.

Choose the strongest applicable published comparison. Identify its version, definitions, assumptions and claim; explain its applicability, limits and incomplete search or access. Map the candidate's objects, inputs or interventions and outputs into that method. Before testing, specify what the mapping must preserve. For equivalence, state and check both directions over the declared domain. Identify distinctions lost in translation.

Check a matched case expected to translate and the smallest case expected to distinguish the approaches. Establish that both tests address the intended criterion. Include limiting or degenerate cases where an apparent match could be vacuous. Hold assumptions and inputs fixed where possible. A case outside a method's assumptions cannot refute it within them. Failure to find a mapping remains unresolved unless a witness or argument rules out the stated mapping class. One counterexample can refute the corresponding universal claim; one successful example cannot prove universal equivalence or validate the proposal.

In the review, mark each required property as covered, with a demonstrated difference under stated assumptions, or unresolved. Give the mapping, witness or scope of the proof, sources, contrary evidence and conditions for reconsideration. Limit novelty to what survives these comparisons. A useful synthesis or application can stand on its own merits. Keep the formal result, literature coverage and programme owner's decision distinct.

## Before handing off

- [ ] Does the comparison use the strongest applicable method under explicit assumptions?
- [ ] Do mappings, matched controls and distinguishing cases test the stated property and domain?
- [ ] Are demonstrated differences, formal coverage, remaining uncertainty and the owner’s research decision distinct?
