# Review the literature reproducibly

Answer a defined scholarly question with a search, selection, appraisal and synthesis that another reader can follow. Publication alone does not establish a claim. Judge source roles and independence, and use the owning tools and permissions for retrieval and saving artifacts.

Before searching, define the question, population or phenomenon, relevant intervention or exposure, comparator, outcomes, study designs, languages, dates, sources and exclusion rules. In the review's research record, keep search locations, exact strategies, dates, amendments and access limits. Deduplicate underlying works rather than links.

Use two-stage screening when volume warrants it. Keep exclusion reasons, conflicts and their resolutions. For included work, extract study identity, design, sample, setting, measures, analysis, finding or effect, uncertainty, funding, limitations, corrections and shared dataset or author histories into the study table.

Judge quality for the claim: design fit, selection and measurement bias, confounding, missing data, multiple comparisons, statistical precision, external validity and reproducibility. Choose narrative, tabular or quantitative synthesis according to comparability. Pooling incompatible constructs cannot produce a defensible single estimate.

Synthesize across studies, preserving direction, magnitude, variation, contradictions, null findings and gaps. Check that each citation supports the sentence and strength claimed.

Deliver the protocol, search and screening records, study table, appraisal, synthesis, limitations, confidence basis and update trigger as the literature review's work products. Stop when the declared search is sufficient for the decision, missing access prevents the conclusion or study differences make the requested conclusion unsupported. Keep studies and extracted facts dated in the evidence record.

## Before handing off

- [ ] Does the question define the search and inclusion scope?
- [ ] Were sources read and their methods appraised before claims were attributed to them?
- [ ] Are strength, disagreement, exclusions and missing coverage visible, with an update trigger?
