# Check a transformed input against its original

Identify the original input, proposed transformed copy, consumer and qualities the owner needs preserved. Use the input-preparation and error-handling methods to review the actual conversion or routing results. In the reply, give findings with their locations, cases covered and the remaining quality, integrity, cancellation or usage checks. Converter selection, transformation and submission follow their own task and permission.
