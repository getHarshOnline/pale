# Plan a recovery rehearsal

Describe the interruption or restore scenario, who owns the affected system and the readiness decision the rehearsal should inform. Choose the relevant checks from the recovery guidance. Name who will run them and who can accept the recovered state for use.

Put the inputs, fresh restore target, required component checks and remaining activation or retirement decisions in the recovery plan. Leave the plan open if nobody has authority to accept the restored state. Planning a check does not establish that it ran or passed.

## Before handing off

- [ ] Verify the inputs and use a fresh restore target.
- [ ] Read back the required components before judging the restore.
- [ ] Name the remaining activation or retirement decision.
