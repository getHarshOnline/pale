# Review a draft and its proposed source use

Identify the candidate and version, known sources and versions, audience, each proposed act and supplied permission references. Use the research guide to establish source identity, origin, applicability, conflicts, gaps and inspected coverage.

Review the draft as a complete arrangement using the source-dependence guide. For quotation, reuse, adaptation, distribution, publication, incorporation into a model or workflow, training, network provision or another governed act, separately assess the rights evidence for each act and component.

Return editorial dependence and use-permission findings separately in the requested review. Independent expression does not grant permission; permission does not establish independent expression. Hold an affected path if a required judgment is unresolved, unsupported, denied or needs a qualified owner. Identify the source, authoring history, Pack, grant, component decision or ruling needed to resume.

The result is a recommendation. Clearance, acceptance of a Pack or Package, publication, distribution, upload, training, network access, source changes and other actions remain separately governed.

## Before handing off

- [ ] Was the work compared with its sources and their roles?
- [ ] Are editorial dependence and permission for the act distinct?
- [ ] Are changes or decisions needed before wider use clear?
