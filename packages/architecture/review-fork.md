# Review a fork and its consumer

Use the version-control method to identify the upstream, mirror, maintained branch and consuming checkout. Compare the observed refs and their dates, ancestry and the transport actually used to reach the consumer.

Return those observations and unresolved divergence or history risks in the review reply. Keep them separate from proposed integration, review or dependency-pin changes. Name who owns the next action, what they are permitted to do and which observations are still missing.

## Before handing off

- [ ] Identify the fork, source version and transport.
- [ ] Compare the objects or changes that reach the intended consumer.
- [ ] Record unresolved divergence or history risk.
