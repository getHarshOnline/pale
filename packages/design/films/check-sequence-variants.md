# Make each page sequence readable on its own

Design a resting still for each beat. It supplies the view before motion starts, the reduced sequence, a narrow treatment when useful, and the document without scripts. Capture a composed endpoint at the time and crop that actually read. An arbitrary paused frame can leave the subject behind a mask, text mid-fade, or the relation unfinished.

| Rendition | Work to do |
| --- | --- |
| Light with motion | Check the rests and the moving frames where the words sit. |
| Light reduced | Step among the resting stills. Carry a transformation through the pair of images and words. Compose the strongest moment with scale, material or light. |
| Dark with motion | Grade stills and films from the dark palette and light. Check that large areas do not glare against dark ground. Derive the light grade from the dark master if that is the project direction. |
| Dark reduced | Judge the dark storyboard as a complete composition. |
| Desktop | Give the whole mechanism room if that helps explain it. Add a pointer reveal only when its content is otherwise available. |
| Handheld | Preserve the subject, hierarchy and meaning of every beat. Reframe as needed. Words can move below the frame. Check safe areas and changing viewport chrome. |

Use the same beat sections in both screen treatments. Choose the image and presentation from the actual space and input capabilities. Reach the same reduced rendition by system preference and the page control. Change the preference during playback and check that it settles without a reload. Test the combined effects of slow connections, failed graphics, missing scripts, contrast settings and forced colors. With no script or a failed film, the resting stills still make a document. Prefer opaque text beds when transparency would lose the read.

## Worked example

A mechanism is wide enough to show three gears at a desk. The handheld edit alternates a close view of the active pair and a resting overview, with the words below the image. The same three beat sections remain. Capture the light and dark versions at rest and during each join. In reduced motion, show the previous and new gear positions beside the same text. Turn scripts off, then simulate a failed video. If any beat loses its claim, fix the still or its text before changing the player.
