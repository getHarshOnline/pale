# Review a proposed evidence update

Use the owner's accepted baseline and criteria for a material change. Apply the evidence and retrieval guides to the new evidence and counterevidence. Return findings for each claim, inspected coverage, remaining gaps and the next useful observation. The owner separately decides whether to accept the update.

## Before handing off

- [ ] Are the earlier claim and its sources identified?
- [ ] Was the new material read for what it actually establishes?
- [ ] Are revised conclusions limited to those that relied on the changed premise?
