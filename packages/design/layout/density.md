# Tune density and the space between things

## Get density and small relationships right

Density is useful meaning per view, not simply a smaller font or less padding. Reading needs a sustained line and relevant apparatus; scanning needs parallel anatomy and clear choices; reference work needs comparable values and adjacent qualifications. Choose from the task. A single staged thesis may serve an opening that would fail as an index withholding its options.

| Task | Composition to establish | Failure to inspect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sustained reading | A measured text column with relevant notes and tools beside it or at an appropriate break. | Callouts and tools interrupt every passage, or a rail competes with the prose. |
| Scanning an index or collection | Parallel units with enough title, qualification and evidence of kind to make a choice. | Equal-sized units obscure real differences, or large empty cards hide the available choices. |
| Comparing reference values | Aligned values, consistent units, labels close to values and qualifications beside them. | Compared values sit in separate boxes, or narrative is forced into reference rows without a comparison task. |

Disclose supporting detail where useful; do not hide the choice set merely to make the page look quiet. A reader should be able to scan an edge or column to find an item without reading every unit in full. Use the role's type floor when composing dense material, and test reader spacing overrides.

Tighten related material while retaining separation between unrelated groups. Keep those differences when columns disappear. Space roles may adapt at different rates; shrinking every distance proportionally can leave excessive section pauses while crushing label/control relationships. Use spacing roles where adopted, fixing recurring recipes rather than adding compensating per-instance overrides. A particular token vocabulary or ban on child margins is not supplied by this method.

Inspect the small bonds that explain belonging: label/control, number/unit, title/metadata, figure/caption, heading/first paragraph, icon/text and source/qualification. Compare each pair's actual gap with the nearest unrelated neighbor, at the tested widths and states. A label closer to another field misleads even when both CSS values came from a scale. Preserve the semantic association too; visual proximity alone does not label a form control.

| Pair | Failure to inspect |
| --- | --- |
| Label and control | The label sits as close to the previous field as to its own. |
| Number and unit | The unit wraps away, floats or changes scale without a meaningful role. |
| Title and metadata | The date, author or kind appears to belong to the next item. |
| Source and qualification | Population, date or method is detached from the figure it limits. |
| Trigger and preview | The opened preview loses its trigger or covers the text being read. |
| Heading and first line | The heading sits as close to the previous section as to its own paragraph. |
| Figure and caption | The caption reads as the start of the following passage. |
| Icon and text | Mathematical centering leaves the icon optically offset, or its gap ignores the type size. |
| Related controls | Gaps within an action group match those between unrelated groups. |

For compared records, align suitable label and value edges and inspect their baselines. Repeated anatomy aids scanning; not every row must become a fixed table. Keep responsive labels, wrapped values and long translations coherent. Inspect focused, pressed and invalid states after an icon, border or error message appears. Browser defaults and exact geometry are hypotheses until the rendered result is checked.

Give large spaces an intelligible role: grouping, reading measure, an expressive pause, a subject's staging, useful apparatus or safe access to a target. A stranded rail, padding-driven scroll or an accidental empty slot may reveal wasted structure. Remove or reassign it where it harms the task. Do not fill it with irrelevant content or erase deliberate asymmetry just to complete a grid. For a scroll-linked sequence, a hold may serve reading time; inspect its content and pacing rather than condemning every unchanged interval.

## Give each gap a job

Separate items inside a group, the group from its neighbors and major sections from each other. Space should make that nesting recognizable without drawing a box for every level. Group a date, version and source as metadata when they describe one item. Give its status a gap or break when it means something different. A narrow screen can shorten pauses between sections while preserving the gap that keeps a value attached to its unit and source.

Read negative space as part of the composition. It can protect a reading field, give one object emphasis, keep an axis legible or leave room for a transition. A dead margin consumes width without doing one of those jobs. Move a supporting annotation into unused space only if its connection to the passage stays obvious. Let the prose keep its measure while figures or apparatus use adjacent tracks. At a narrow width move the apparatus back to the passage or a clearly labeled disclosure.

Keep only the apparatus the reader needs beside an ongoing passage. A rail that accumulates contents, related items, notes, discussion and subscription prompts can become a second page with a long empty tail. Place other material at a useful end point or command surface. Give a needed rail enough width for its contents and let the text use the remaining space; when both no longer fit, recompose instead of squeezing the reading column. Close the gap left by relocated apparatus. If one opening column runs much longer than its neighbor, compare continuing its material below the opening row rather than leaving the short column stranded.

Large gaps need reading, staging or pacing reasons, but their size alone does not establish a failure. Restore a missing pause without adding uniform padding everywhere. Where material and light are part of the direction, an empty region still shows that ground. For a pinned sequence, account for the whole scroll distance through changing frames and deliberate reading holds; an unexplained stretch is different from a held phrase.

## Worked example

A list’s date is closer to the next record’s title than to its own. Bring the date closer to its title and keep a larger gap between records. Keep label and value gaps tighter than spacing between groups. Compare in reading, scanning and reference contexts. The reference view may carry several aligned values with units. The reading view can keep apparatus beside the paragraph. Preserve room around each actual input target.

## Sources for the decision

- [NN/g: proximity](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/gestalt-proximity/) and [progressive disclosure](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/progressive-disclosure/): grouping through distance and revealing supporting detail without concealing the available choices.
- [Carbon spacing](https://carbondesignsystem.com/elements/spacing/overview/) and [Utopia fluid space](https://utopia.fyi/blog/designing-with-a-fluid-space-palette/): examples of stepped roles and independently changing space pairs.
- [Every Layout: Sidebar](https://every-layout.dev/layouts/sidebar/) and [Axioms](https://every-layout.dev/rudiments/axioms/): preserve the reading measure while letting neighboring content use available space.
- [Apple layout guidance](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/layout) and [Practical Typography: page layout](https://practicaltypography.com/maxims-of-page-layout.html): margins and spacing relationships across contexts.
- [W3C: information and relationships](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/info-and-relationships.html) and [text spacing](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/text-spacing.html): structural associations and the spacing changes a dense composition must survive.
- [Practical Typography: whitespace characters](https://practicaltypography.com/white-space-characters.html): textual spacing that keeps a number with its unit across outputs.
