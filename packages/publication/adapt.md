# Adapt accepted content for another medium

Identify the accepted source version, approved claims, destination limits and intended reader action. Preserve support and qualifications for each claim. If an essential qualification cannot fit, change the framing or leave that variant unapproved. Return identified variants and explain material differences. Scheduling and delivery remain with their owning systems and permissions.

## Before handing off

- [ ] Are the approved source and destination's reading or viewing task clear?
- [ ] Do claims, qualifications and voice survive the change of form?
- [ ] Was the actual variant read, with unresolved constraints identified?
