# Preserve what the supplied asset already gets right

## Preserve what is already right

When only one region needs correction, prefer an appropriate local edit or composite to regenerating the entire accepted composition. State which features may change. Check the whole result: an edit can change identity, lettering, proportions, lighting or pixels outside the requested area. Use [Choose and diagnose a generation method](../generate.md) when a generator is involved; its controls offer different kinds of conditioning, with different limits.

At working size, inspect silhouette, anatomy or geometry, contact, perspective, material response and coherent illumination. At full size, inspect edges, small lettering, repeated detail, texture artifacts and invented objects. At delivery size, check that the intended subject and hierarchy still read. A thumbnail can hide a defective hand; a technically sharp full-size image can become unreadable in its actual placement.

Compose for the actual destination. Reserve usable space for overlaid words when the design needs it, and inspect the real words against the real image. Do not require an empty text field in work that has no overlay. A wide and a tall delivery can need different compositions; stretching or cropping a master is suitable only when it preserves the intended relation and subject.

## Worked example

A source photograph needs a wider placement. Protect the subject’s face and the original grade. Make the crop first and compare how the expansion meets the existing edges. Check the export’s metadata and keep the original available to its owner. A reused source and a generated reconstruction make different claims and need different labels.
