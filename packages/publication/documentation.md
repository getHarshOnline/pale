# Write for the reader's actual job

Use for product help, developer documentation, explanatory material or exact reference. Study the selected good examples for their language, structure and reader continuity. Apply their useful principles to the actual subject; borrowing a hosting technology or heading pattern does not reproduce the quality.

## Distinguish the job before choosing the home

Product help enables someone to understand and use a product. Installation, startup, a CLI command, editor operation and agent connection can all be product tasks despite containing technical details. Developer documentation enables integration, extension, modification and maintenance. Its introduction still needs a concise account of what the system does, why it exists and a useful example. It should not assume the reader already understands the product.

Generated API or schema reference is a form within developer documentation, not an additional audience. The native declaration or generator owns derived signatures and structures; author useful introductions, examples and explanations around them rather than hand-editing generated facts. Private IA retains internal meaning, decisions, evidence and owner links. The project determines actual Content, repository, collection, hostname and publication bindings. Do not infer them from the presence of code or impose another project's routes universally.

Different explanations can serve different reader questions without being redundant. Maintain each actual procedure, interface fact and decision once with its owner, then crosslink where needed. Do not remove necessary orientation or make a public manual depend on private context for normal use.

Choose the page's primary reader question and order before organising its source material. Open with the result or question, then the concept needed to understand or perform the work. Introduce internal structures when they explain that mechanism or decision, rather than leading with a generator's vocabulary or maintenance boundaries. Link another reader job at its actual handoff; do not make one page perform every audience's setup, integration and exhaustive lookup.

## Complete the requested kind of explanation

For a tutorial, carry one meaningful activity through a complete supported input, the actual action, observable result and next change. Give prerequisites where needed, define the example's variables and identify the files the reader edits. A disposable fixture can demonstrate a test but cannot supply a continuing workspace. Explain persistence or cleanup when it affects the reader's next step. Use expected results and relevant failure/recovery guidance so the reader can recognize success and recover. Internal test history does not substitute for the task itself.

For a procedure, start from the reader's current situation and needed result. Keep the task intact across a prerequisite detour. If product startup requires a developer source build, link that precise setup and return to the same product task afterwards. Do not send the reader to a general manual and make them reconstruct the missing connection. A complete code-integration example similarly needs defined inputs, the supported call and an interpretable result.

For a concept or architecture explanation, develop the mechanism, responsibilities, reasoning, alternatives and useful limits at the reader's depth. Follow a concrete change through the system when it makes the explanation clearer. Repository layout helps locate code; it does not explain why the system works. For reference, make the exact subject and applicable version easy to find, explain behavior and link a meaningful example. Neither a concept nor a lookup page needs a compulsory tutorial checklist. Do not generate every content type for every feature.

Keep exhaustive format admission, field tables and precise compatibility in lookup material, linked where the detail becomes necessary. A reader's procedure is not a fixture catalogue, test journal or release-review packet. Keep those records with their maintained source and evidence owners. Preserve the examples, reasoning and useful depth that answer the page's question. Group alternative paths or disclose optional detail when that helps the reader choose and continue; components and a shorter page cannot repair a confused explanation.

Put current availability and consequential limits beside the affected behavior or choice. Explain their practical consequence there; do not repeat every proof, account or release qualification throughout unrelated steps. Preserve historical or proposed status without inventing operational onboarding. A narrow runnable feature can support a product explanation, but should not replace its broader purpose merely because that feature is easy to demonstrate.

## Follow the actual reader path

When reducing an existing page, use the diff to compare every removed reason, tradeoff and example with the original. Have a reader who understands the subject check that its useful meaning survives in the finished account or a link to its owning explanation. Keep this comparison in the existing review.

Check the page from an ordinary entrypoint and a direct search arrival. Follow the required links rather than assuming a configured address works. Where product help and developer depth are separate, verify the precise task-to-prerequisite handoff and a purposeful return. Test the selected local preview and public route independently when both are required; a literal served link, a symbolic reference and automatic cross-output translation are different mechanisms. Use the smallest native mechanism that resolves the demonstrated gap.

Read the complete rendered body in its intended order, including examples and qualifications. A skim of headings, a source hash or separate section checks do not establish a coherent explanation. Without supplying missing context, can the reader explain the mechanism, perform the task or find the answer with the stated inputs and recognize the result? Preserve useful depth while removing internal qualification machinery from that path. Structural link checks establish surviving links, not missing journeys or good writing. A successful build does not establish this reader outcome. Use actual task observations where a usability claim depends on them and keep unobserved hosting, behavior and approval open.
