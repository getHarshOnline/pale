# Make and check a still or keyframe

Work from rough frames and a small style test before committing full-size images. A page sequence needs its resting frame and whatever entry and exit frames the edit calls for. Compose corresponding frames together. A match on shape needs agreement in position and size. A lens block needs the occluding object on both sides. An intentional cut may depend on a visible difference. Keep the chosen recurring-subject references and nearby approved frame in reach.

When a localized flaw remains, edit the affected region and inspect what stayed the same. Regenerating the whole frame reopens its settled decisions. Restart the composition if the composition itself is wrong. Record which source was edited and what the edit was intended to change.

| Check at full size | Look for |
| --- | --- |
| Treatment and identity | The medium, palette, light, lens, face, costume, proportions and recurring material agree with the intended set. |
| Anatomy and objects | Check fingers, eyes, limbs, stray props and duplicated objects. |
| Lettering | Check every intended word and look for garbled glyphs, fake signatures and unintended labels. |
| Reading plane | Test the words where they appear in each layout. Keep their contrast without hiding the subject under a rescue overlay. If words sit elsewhere the image needs no reserved text field. |
| Source and continuity | State whether the image is illustrative or documentary. Compare its light, object position and joining shape to adjacent frames. |

## Choose the frame and test the edit before spending on a series

Sketch a few low-cost alternatives first. Choose where the reader's eye lands, what the subject looks like at rest and which shapes the next frame will need to match. When a still is a transition endpoint, keep its direction, crop, silhouette and light compatible with the intended neighbor. A good isolated still can fail that join. If the image is for a reading plane, test the words on its actual plate, including transparency and the most difficult area of the frame.

For a local correction, identify what may change and compare what should stay after the edit. A masked generation can alter background pixels at the join. Preserve the accepted original and use a composite when the surrounding image must stay fixed. Inspect full size for repeated geometry, unwanted text, broken anatomy, clipped highlights and the important source details. Repeat with the delivery crop and decode before calling the still ready.

## Worked example

A still shows a labelled optical assembly. One invented mark appears on a finished panel. Mask just the mark and use the approved material as a reference. Inspect the same region at full size and verify the label, lighting and bolts elsewhere have not changed. For a poster crop, put the title on open ground. For a narrow page, put it below the image if putting it over the panel would obscure the assembly.
