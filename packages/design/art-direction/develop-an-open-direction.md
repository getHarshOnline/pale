# Use a reference board to develop an open direction

Use a board when the direction still needs to be chosen. Gather real references first. Record the quality each supplies and the rights to reuse or upload it. A reconstruction brief calls for studying the actual template or sequence. An accepted direction can go straight to the work it governs.

## Gather, compare and turn choices into a treatment

Choose one image that carries the question and neighbors that show how far the treatment can range. Ten to fifteen images and two to five terms can be a starting size for an exploratory board. Use fewer for a specific reconstruction and spend the time on details and frames. Name what each source shows: raking light, a readable text plane, the shape of a gesture, or how a color appears next to the stock. Show a near miss when it explains an important difference. A direction can hold tensions such as quiet reading and dense information.

When several directions are credible, build two or three coherent boards that vary the actual approach. A light and dark grade of the same design rarely answers this question. After choosing, keep the board’s decisions and any useful facets for material, light, type or people. Make a cheap study on the production path before locking an asset set. Turn the qualities that matter into concrete decisions about surface, light, palette, crop and viewpoint. Carry any agreed reconstruction or reuse with its own fidelity and rights.

Use the project’s source library for originals and the project’s working format for links and notes. Before using a hosted board or generator, check whether the assets can be uploaded and what the service can do with them.

## Worked example: make “warm and precise” observable

Take the matte edge and shallow shadow from a paper reference and the numerical alignment from an instrument. Give the reading field warm stock and let a cool mark indicate a measurement. Try an alternate board where the precision comes from geometry and typography. Compare the two on the same real content. If the paper image’s main quality came from texture under a raking light, check that effect without making the numbers harder to read. Keep the selected source and the choice that made it useful.
