# Evaluate a candidate without changing it

Judge one fixed candidate against the accepted rubric and supplied evidence. Leave the artifact unchanged and assess it independently of the generator's conclusion.

Report material gaps with a concrete trigger, supporting evidence and consequence. Distinguish verified defects, supported concerns, untested claims and preferences. Apply the actual audience, constraints and quality standard; effort and apparent sophistication do not establish quality.

Return a result for each criterion with citations, confidence and the findings in the existing review response. A pass means the evidence supports the rubric. Plausibility alone is insufficient. Stay within the brief, take no consequential action, and do not invent findings to make the review look thorough.
