# Critique the design against its intended outcome

Judge the exact plan before implementation and the exact rendered outcome after implementation. Read the artifact and original evidence before detector scores or the author's conclusions. Check what the audience can perceive, understand and do. Preserve useful reconstruction or reuse for the role the reference was supplied to serve. Challenge missing outcomes and requirements unsupported by owner intent.

For an overview, inspect what the audience can independently retell about the action, result and why it matters before supplying the explanation. Reconcile that account with the identified product sources. Judge each applicable medium in its native presentation. A film verdict needs the exact encode, playback, transitions, applicable sound and embedding context for the claim. A plan verdict and a rendered verdict describe different evidence.

Read [Recognize unconsidered design choices](art-direction/recognize-defaults.md) for the concrete tells, their corrections and the cases that justify a familiar technique. Name the category’s usual page and its predictable opposite, then check whether this brief actually earns either. Keep the reasons with the finding, without turning a tell count into a verdict.

## Judge the intended reader and whole treatment

Distinguish a product user's operating task, a developer's extension or reference task, an audience-facing publication and an internal qualification record before judging the content. Engine names, commands, schemas and implementation detail can be essential in technical documentation. They can distract when substituted for an audience's actual job. Internal test status cannot replace a usable instruction or an observed reader outcome. Necessary attribution, methodology, limitations and illustration labels remain where readers need them; do not remove them as production commentary. Exact documentation surfaces and routes belong to the project, not to this role.

Record what the actual artifact communicates independently of detector scores. Then reconcile it with the original request, corrections, accepted direction and selected reference roles before forming a verdict. For a rendered verdict, write that source-based judgment before opening the builder’s plan or detector output. Use those afterward to locate causes or reconcile a finding, preserving what the artifact actually showed. A first impression helps expose unintended prominence; it does not outrank the brief. Determine whether the complete treatment serves this subject and task. Familiar controls, appropriate templates and deliberately reconstructed detail need not be unique. Replacing the copy with an unrelated subject can expose an unexamined visual argument, but shared construction surviving that experiment is not itself a failure.

Judge a plan for decisions and missing intent, not for rendered behavior it cannot establish. Inspect the actual target at ordinary viewing scale for an output claim. Choose the affected page families, awkward content and relevant conditions from the decision being reviewed; do not turn a local repair into an automatic full-site campaign. A component specimen proves the specimen, not adoption by the whole publication. A scene frame proves neither complete playback nor sound.

Check the intended hierarchy, grouping and state before crediting polish. Beautiful material can conceal an inert control or missing relation. Sparse, dense, centered, asymmetric, dark, monospace, translucent or cinematic treatment is not inherently good or bad. Examine what the technique does for this brief and whether its qualifications and access survive. Common patterns are leads for inquiry, not forbidden shapes or an aesthetic scoring formula.

Use [Judge the design through its working parts](inspect/lenses.md) for the lens questions and the contrary-evidence pass.

## Locate the failure and preserve the other side

Distinguish missing intended presence, excessive competition and the wrong kind of expression. If the accepted character is absent, another restraint rule does not deliver it. If effects compete with the task, reduce the competing treatment before deleting useful information. If the wrong axis carries the idea, change that axis instead of merely making the same device louder or quieter. Recheck the quality on the other side so a repair does not overshoot.

Diagnose at the owning level: shared direction or identity, recurring component or recipe, one composition, or local polish. A world-level mismatch cannot be repaired by repeated spacing adjustments; one line-break defect does not reopen the whole identity. Keep an unclear cause as a hypothesis. Compare the actual before and after when a constraint has been interpreted by subtraction: freezing architecture does not necessarily freeze prototype pixels, and asking for less churn does not mean removing the selected type or material.

Look for evidence of decisions: information has intelligible weight; small label/control and value/unit relationships hold; real states are visible; the surface responds in its declared role; and the intended reference quality reaches actual consumers. A placeholder should not masquerade as proof, a decorative readout as a measurement, or a proposed state as a working service. Conceptual illustration and authored UI can be valid when their role is clear.

Use diagnostic experiments only where they answer a question. Removing an apparently unnecessary divider or badge can reveal whether grouping still works; restore it when it carried useful meaning. Suppressing an accent can expose identity carried only by color when broader distinction is required. Neither experiment creates a quota to remove approved detail or make every primitive different. Keep the few useful refused alternatives and their reasons with the existing review, rather than building a new universal rejection register.

## Review temporal qualities in their actual form

Observe motion at ordinary speed for feel and at slowed or stepped playback when diagnosing origin, phase, overshoot, handoff or a loop seam. Input-driven motion needs its relevant interruption; a scrubbed sequence needs both directions and settled positions; an authored film needs its selected full timeline and applicable sound. Do not apply drawer reversal tests to a film's story.

For a reference-led sequence, compare the opening, central change and payoff against the whole requested outcome, not only the builder's device checklist. Reproducing each local trick can still yield one repetitive staging or a story whose labels do all the explanatory work. Keep necessary reader qualifications and the intended viewing window visible. A running prototype, encoded delivery and browser embedding establish different claims. Use a fresh look or independent review when tuning has dulled judgment; a compulsory waiting period is not an acceptance criterion.

## Explain the finding and what the verdict covers

State the exact location and condition, what is observed, its evidence, the intended quality or reader task that fails, its likely owning correction and what already works and must survive. Use the native review format; a new fixed field packet is not required. Replace adjectives such as cheap or generic with the observable choices behind the reaction. Separate a measured value from a visual judgment and both from an untested explanation.

Prioritize by real consequence, recurrence and persistence after identifying the finding. Do not inflate severity to look thorough or soften an unmet required outcome because its label is low priority. Recheck applicable carried findings against the changed artifact before declaring them closed; a revised plan or new rule body does not prove that an existing output changed. Preserve valid unaffected findings and positive qualities.

Return the verdict in the existing review: the revision, located findings and observations, useful alternatives considered, qualities to preserve and conditions left unreviewed. State whether it is blocked on named requirements or ready for the owner’s review. Attribute self-review honestly and use the selected independent route when requested or required. An untested explanation remains a hypothesis; test it against contrary evidence before closing the finding. The owner retains acceptance and publication decisions.
