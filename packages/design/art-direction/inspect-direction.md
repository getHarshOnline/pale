# Judge the whole treatment at delivery scale

## Verify the direction

Use [Recognize unconsidered design choices](recognize-defaults.md) when the treatment feels generic, timid, excessive or unlike the selected references. Check each tell against its purpose and justified exceptions before making a finding.

When asked for a direction or plan, identify the states that will need inspection. When verifying implementation, inspect the changed states and affected journeys for a repair, or representative states for a new direction: first view, core task, dense data, narrow viewport, error, empty, and completion. Check hierarchy, legibility, accessibility, content resilience, responsive transitions, input behavior, and performance as applicable. Locate a discrepancy in the concept, token model, component, or content.

Keep the affected decision open when the product purpose or who can decide it is unresolved, the sources cannot support a direction, access would be lost, the memorable element competes with the task or implementation needs an architectural change outside the authorization. Continue independent work that does not depend on that decision. In the existing review or reply, explain the chosen treatment, useful rejected alternatives, constraints, observed states, exceptions and the fact or owner decision still needed. Keep the accepted direction with the product that owns it.

## Worked example

Compare an opening at phone and desktop widths. Hide the accent temporarily and identify the subject by composition, type and material. Exercise the first action. If the composition looks precise in a capture while the actual task is hard to locate, fix the hierarchy or control instead of lowering the visual review standard.
