# Assess a product need

Identify who has the problem, what they are trying to do, the difficulty they encounter, their workaround and its observable consequence. Separate what they requested from the need you observed, the solution proposed and the product owner's decision. Compare other explanations and the smallest plausible alternative.

In your reply, recommend whether to proceed, investigate, reframe or decline. Give the evidence for that recommendation and what could change it.

## Before handing off

- [ ] Does the finding identify the person, task and observed problem?
- [ ] Have other plausible explanations been compared with the observations?
- [ ] Is the next question small enough to test and capable of changing the decision?
