# Give the next worker enough context to continue

Use this when another session, role, agent host or owner must continue a particular task. The handoff carries the work and existing permission; acceptance, delivery and any further action still need to be established through their own records.

Name the goal, recipient, scope and exclusions. Point to the source that owns the intent, the files or system that hold current state, and whoever can authorise the next consequential action. Distinguish observations, proposals, assumptions and historical context. Keep parallel work and different owners clear so the recipient can continue without colliding with another writer.

In the requested brief or handoff message, include what the recipient needs:

- the exact revision or local state and how to reproduce it;
- completed work and its checks;
- failed approaches and what failed;
- corrections that replace earlier instructions;
- uncommitted or concurrent work that must be preserved;
- unresolved questions and who can answer them;
- the next useful action and any prerequisite;
- the check that will establish the result; and
- the change that would make this handoff stale.

For independent review, supply the original request and later corrections separately from the candidate. For each required input, identify what it contributes, the exact source or section and how the candidate handles it. Distinguish choosing a tool or method, running it and using its result. Give a delegated role only the sources, permitted actions and budget it needs.

Link to sources and state settled decisions instead of copying entire documents. Include private material only when the recipient may access it and needs it for the task. Keep credentials, unrelated personal data and unrelated conversation out of the handoff.

Make important claims clear as established, observed, proposed, unresolved or superseded. A draft may still need acceptance; a written file may still need installation, activation, publication or actual use. Preserve a conflict and its sources instead of choosing the newer-looking wording or blending a correction with the account it replaces.

Finish when the recipient can identify the next action, its permission and the result to check. If ownership is unresolved, state cannot be reproduced, disclosure is unsafe or the handoff would imply permission never given, resolve that issue before handing over the affected part. Saving or sending the brief, accepting it, changing work status and implementing it remain distinct actions.
