# Choose a construction for the visible change

Use this repertoire to compare treatments in the existing shot plan. It supplies options, not a sequence to fill or a quota of effects. Choose HTML, vectors, canvas or 3D according to the chosen mechanism and the project’s renderer.

| What should become perceptible | Construction to try | Decisions the shot must make |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One object continues while its function changes | Carrier morph, button-to-player, shape or container match | Corresponding edges, changing contents, mask containment and material continuity |
| A part becomes an environment | Zoom-through, window-to-world, continuous camera passage | Geography, entry scale, occlusion, exit landmark and legible arrival |
| Travel explains a system | Shared scene, camera keyframes, context pullback | Meaningful positions, path, scale and what stays visible; a node diagram is only one option |
| A structure becomes visible | Exploded layers, depth separation, controlled orbit | Why each plane exists, its contents, perspective, labels and recombination |
| Attention moves among alternatives | Screenshot fan, focus plane, lens, split-to-one | Comparative meaning, readable scale, focal priority and retained context |
| A real interface demonstrates an action | Establish, isolate and return; reveal a result or expand a component | Verified labels and state, visible action and contextual return |
| Type carries an argument | Masked title, word or line reveal, spatial reflow, anchored emphasis | Exact copy, line breaks, role, arrival, stable reading interval and departure |
| A source passage becomes evidence | Document view, whole-line mark, move toward the passage | Verbatim text, attribution, mark timing, legible rest and source continuity |
| An image becomes a scene | Slat or mask reveal, bounded wipe, crop or cutaway | Useful region, material edge, meaning of the transition and destination framing |
| Speech and graphics explain together | Phrase anchors, progressive detail, captions, timed props | Acoustic onset, phrase meaning, safe reading field and disclosure order |
| A quantity changes or alternatives compare | Count, bars-to-line morph, comparison, whole-unit figure | Actual values, units, meaning of interpolation and qualifications; an animation must not invent a trend |
| A process or relationship needs a diagram | Path, staircase, pipeline, funnel or hub | Whether that model expresses the actual relationship |

If every idea becomes a path, node or card, return to the perceptual change and compare another construction. If only the palette changed while framing, glass and hierarchy stayed fixed, decide whether the brief asked for that reconstruction or needs a fuller adaptation. A camera touring an unchanged slide may need a better focal change or a deliberate hold. A production rule useful for one generated shot must not suppress coordinated authored choreography.

Keep camera, object, mask and protected type on separate controls where they need independent timing. Inspect intermediate frames as well as endpoints: a correct finish can conceal clipping or a disappearing material during the join. Presenter framing comes from the shot, not a tool’s default. When identical labels name successive inputs and outputs, try keeping the labels still while the actual result changes beneath them. Keep the subject as context when opening evidence about it, and distinguish editorial labels from controls still active in the captured interface.

## Worked example

A report changes from a total to the transactions that explain it. Compare a camera move through decorative nodes with a fixed view that expands the actual rows beneath the unchanged total. Choose the view that lets the audience connect the amount and its parts at the delivered size. If a spatial move explains a real hierarchy the fixed view cannot show, record that reason in [Plan a shot from both ends](frame-a-shot.md), including the simpler alternative and the reading hold.
