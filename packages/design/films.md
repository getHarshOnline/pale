# Compose a sequence people can follow

Use this for an authored film or visual sequence. Study the original request, corrections and reference roles. Decide what the audience should understand, recognize or experience. Carry that into the shots and judge their relationship in connected playback.

An approved identity supplies character, not a mandatory layout, diagram, camera distance or pacing template. Combine the audience’s task, source claims, reference mechanism, composition, type, material, motion and sound into the film’s own treatment. Choose the visual mechanism for the change it makes understandable. Count every reading task in a beat, including text inside screenshots, and leave time for the action and its consequence to register. When a treatment adds substantial machinery, compare a simpler viable way to show the same thing.

- [ ] Does the cut deliver the audience outcome and the qualities selected from its references?
- [ ] Are the subject, reading plane and hold clear through each change?
- [ ] Do the clock and placement fit the intended sequence?
- [ ] Does the delivered cut preserve the qualities observed in the plan?
