# Frame the design problem before choosing a form

## Establish the design problem

Name the user, their task, the product promise, the emotional posture, the content density, the devices and input modes, the existing brand evidence, and the technical constraints. Separate facts from aspirations. An adjective such as “premium” or “clean” is not direction until it changes typography, space, hierarchy, color, imagery, motion, or interaction behavior in a defensible way.

Read the original brief and the design references before making the visual decision. Trace the relevant principles to the existing composition, type, spacing, interaction and components. For a focused repair, reuse accepted direction and patterns. Read the owners of the changed surface and the dependencies it affects. Derive a new system or produce a separate design artifact when that is the requested output. When implementation is requested, apply the decision in the surface and inspect the affected rendered states.

When choosing new direction, name the primary organizing idea and the hierarchy of its contrasts. The organizing idea should explain the surface at a glance; a controlled contrast creates recognition without turning every element into a signature. Compare the direction with at least one credible alternative. In the existing brief or design reply, explain why the chosen treatment serves the product and task better. A simpler treatment is a useful comparison when it could carry the same meaning.

## Read settled choices and open only the questions that matter

Start from the original request, its corrections, the approved examples and the source that owns the claims. For a repair, name what changes and what the current request preserves. For a new direction, ask only about questions whose answers change the next step. Keep the person's terms for the task, references and refusals, and label assumptions when a reasonable default lets work proceed.

Fill a gap through a short conversation, using related questions together and reflecting the answer before asking more. Keep original requests and superseding corrections readable beside the derived brief, with quotations, interpretations and defaults distinguishable. Preserve why a person selected a reference; liking its light is different from authorizing reconstruction of its layout. A current correction governs the affected decision, while the original still records what was asked before.

The brief should make the output, audience, useful change, source authority, refusals and success test clear enough to choose a route. Include the medium and clock, delivery surface, material constraints and any rights, privacy or budget limits that affect the choice. Keep a routine default as the agent's assumption within existing authority. If a choice is reserved for the owner, state the concrete choice and its dependency rather than treating silence as acceptance.

For a product overview, read the product account, the intended audience copy and observed behavior. State the job the audience should understand and the visible result that supports it. Distinguish the leading claim, what proves it and what belongs in a companion guide. A verified feature earns screen time when it helps explain the product's use, and a missing demonstration does not authorize inventing one. For media delivery, include the intended player or destination and any separate cover or placement needs.

Identify the capability groups that matter to this audience and the work the product replaces or connects. If a material group is deferred from the overview, say where it will be explained. For a film, distinguish the owner's chosen direction from the timing, guide audio or runtime of a study. Carry the decisions that actually govern the master to its native production plan and reviewer, together with the originals rather than only the builder's interpretation.

## Worked example

A table needs a better path for comparing two observations. Begin with the decisions people make from the comparison and what they must be able to read on a phone. Use the project’s quiet editorial direction for the surrounding account and reserve a strong emphasis for the actual result. Compare this with a version that starts from the available card component. Use whichever supports the reading task.
