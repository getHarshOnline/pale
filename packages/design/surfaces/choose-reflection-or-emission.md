# Separate a reflection from a state light

## Distinguish the visual role from a claim about state

Determine whether light describes a depicted scene, expresses the identity's atmosphere, separates surfaces or signals a real interface state. A lamp in a film need not correspond to runtime computation. Conversely, a glowing process indicator should not imply an active connection or running work that does not exist. Preserve actual selection, progress and completion semantics; material cannot establish a fact, permission, measurement or where the source came from.

Where the accepted interface uses light to express held state, identify that state and the actual carrier. Inspect its entry, held and released forms. A marker that remains lit after deselection or completion is stale feedback. Release may use the established decay, but must not block a new state or input. Separate editorial importance from current runtime state. A featured item need not claim the system is doing something with it.

Keep the design's expressive presence as well as its limits. If it requires perceptible state light, verify it on the real page rather than substituting a quiet weight change. Another design may correctly use text, shape or a flat marker. A project can name its allowed emitters, its material roles and how current location looks. Do not invent a state just to justify decoration or apply an interface rule to a documentary photograph's lighting.

## Draw the room and the held state separately

Environmental light makes materials and edges understandable. Choose a consistent direction for highlights and shadows across the interface. Low-angle light can reveal grain, a machined edge or a change of plane. It should follow the surface rather than its editorial importance. A supplied photograph or authored scene keeps its own light. Apply the interface key to the surrounding plate.

State light answers what the system is holding now. Give it a small brighter core, a falloff attached to that core, and a readable matte form when released. A tint at the bed's luminance can be an accent without reading as light. Test the lit and released form side by side, with color subdued, in each supported color mode. Pair the drawing with text or a programmatically available state. Where the effect has a decay, release toward the matte form, let a new state take over during the decay, and keep input available. A strong bloom belongs to a meaningful peak and must leave nearby text readable.

## Worked example

A toggle shows an edge catching the key light on hover. Its held state lights a small mark and adds an announced state. Keep the mark steady while held. When the state releases, let the mark return to matte without blocking new input. A depicted lamp can stay in the scene independently. Check that no light claims a process is running when the service says it stopped.
