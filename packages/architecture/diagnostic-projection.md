# Review a live status view

## Bind the stream and the viewer

Identify the producer, who can establish each assertion, viewer, host and owner of any embedded panel. Specify the stream, revision, viewer scope, permitted purpose, initial snapshot and resume rules. Supplying a display slot gives the host neither stream credentials nor ownership of data or permission to act. If it can request an action, test that separately authorised route and inspect its actual result.

Authenticate before sending a snapshot or update. Send only information the viewer may receive. Check data, counts, health, errors, sequence gaps and resource signals against visibility rules. Hiding a field after delivery does not prevent its disclosure to the renderer.

## Preserve evidence and lifecycle in the view

Use the same assertion identity and supporting revision in overview, counts, legends, details and action results. For a relationship, identify endpoints, direction, kind and source of evidence and any measured uncertainty. Distinguish a record-level score, a directly observed pair and an inferred relationship. Repeated delivery adds no new evidence. Matching labels or one endpoint do not prove a pair. Compatibility labels must not imply stronger support than the observations provide.

Within disclosure rules, distinguish verified empty results, missing or unmeasured results, stale evidence, disconnection and denied access. An unresolved gap must not look empty or successful. Show unsupported actions as unavailable. A failed embedded panel must not take down separately owned host views. Test visible status and its accessible equivalent.

## Check snapshot, replay and failure boundaries

Tie the resume cursor to viewer scope and stream position. Test duplicates, reordering, gaps, expired cursors, restart, backpressure and scope changes. If exact replay is impossible, require an explicit reset or newly permitted snapshot. Recheck permission on resume and test mid-stream revocation. An old snapshot or late update must not restore a removed record or revoked access.

Use two viewer scopes, a shared identifier, a hidden assertion and a mismatched pair observation. Compare overview, details, counts and accessible status with the owning records, then remove or disconnect the embedded panel. Separately test the live stream, action results and any scale claim with fixed fixtures, target environment, load and budget. Renderer fixtures and authenticated live execution prove different things. Put repeatable cases in the view's tests and report mismatches, coverage and the owner of each remaining check in the review reply.

## Before handing off

- [ ] Check which viewer can see each item and why.
- [ ] Compare the display with the observed cause and recovery state.
- [ ] Keep sensitive or unavailable details out of the wrong view.
