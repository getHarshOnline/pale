# Compare different views of a consequential choice

Start with the choice, its decision-maker, options, shared evidence, criteria and reasons to revisit it. Apply the council method using perspectives that contribute different knowledge or responsibilities. Keep their initial positions independent and retain objections that must be resolved before proceeding.

Return one advisory decision record with the recommendation, reasoning, accepted trade-off, dissent, uncertainty and triggers for reconsideration. If one person or model considered every perspective, say that it was a single review exploring several views. Describe a meeting, an accepted decision or an action as having happened only when it did. The recommendation remains separate from the owner's decision and permission to act.

## Before handing off

- [ ] Is the choice clear, with its decision-maker named?
- [ ] Does the record keep the strongest opposing arguments and their assumptions?
- [ ] Can the reader find the recommendation, dissent and reasons to revisit it?
