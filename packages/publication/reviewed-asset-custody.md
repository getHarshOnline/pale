# Keep the reviewed asset through storage and selection

Use this guide to check archive installation or a change in the selected asset. Editorial meaning and wording have their own review.

Associate the review, render record, observations and any owner acceptance with the candidate's result record. Before installing the archive or issuing its record, compare copied review and evidence with those identities. Compare each against the accepted record, since two mutable files could agree after both were replaced. For historical readback, check acceptance against the digest named in the original review and preserve its scope.

Before changing the selection index, read back installed media and the owner's acceptance record against the identities approved for that selection. Test substitution between review and copying, replacement after staging and a record changing during promotion. On mismatch, keep the previous selection and use the system's recovery route to verify media and index. In the check result, preserve evidence of incomplete rollback. Archive integrity and selection do not grant release permission; the owning system supplies schema, commit and recovery.

## Before handing off

- [ ] Does the installed asset match the reviewed version?
- [ ] Were the actual destination and changed renditions checked?
- [ ] Is it clear who can decide to replace the asset?
