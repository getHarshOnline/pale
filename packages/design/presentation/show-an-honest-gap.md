# Keep the task available when information is missing

## Design honest gaps and appropriate fallbacks

Unknown, absent, zero, unavailable and intentionally withheld are different states. Use the source's vocabulary and permitted disclosure: a title-only record, omitted count, qualified unavailable destination or clearly labeled missing media can be accurate. Plausible sample values, stock images presented as the real subject, fake counters and an em dash read as zero cannot supply missing evidence. Withheld private facts need not be advertised as missing.

Compose with the minimal real record as well as the richest one. A missing portrait need not stop independent layout work or another family of pages; hold only the dependent claim and avoid falsely representing a placeholder as approved production content. Conversely, the existence of a title does not promise a complete available destination. Historical source material remains dated or is withheld from a current claim; faithful rendering of a superseded bundle is still a wrong current representation.

Keep the gap understandable in its actual form. In a description list, a public “not supplied” value can retain the term and its place; a related-items section with no supplied relations can be omitted. Do not fill it with recent or random records. Exercise missing date, missing unit and unavailable destination separately from a genuine zero. Check that no machine-readable output quietly fills a blank with the build date or an inferred value. Keep a deliberately withheld field within its disclosure rules even if a visible placeholder would make the layout easier.

Keep reader-relevant attribution, methodology, derivation, edition context and qualifications where they explain the work. Internal fixture classification, review logistics and design-production commentary belong with their native records unless the audience actually needs them. A synthetic or illustrative example must carry the qualification necessary to avoid misleading the reader; do not erase it under a blanket rule against production notes. Disclosure, confidentiality and publication approval remain owner decisions.

For a publication offering progressive enhancement, preserve a useful document when optional script, GPU effects or fonts are unavailable. Essential facts and paths should not exist only in hover, hue, an animation frame or generated CSS content. An encoded story has its own approved frames, captions or transcript; it does not need to become an interactive DOM object. An application whose core task requires a capability needs an honest unavailable state, not a false promise that every task works without script. Use the actual delivery contract.

## Worked example

An index has a summary and a record that is available to the reader, but its image has no public variant. Keep the title, qualifying text and destination. If a number cannot be computed from the source, leave it unavailable rather than presenting a plausible default. A failure to draw a chart can still leave a useful table.
