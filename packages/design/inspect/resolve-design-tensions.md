# Find what a visual repair is trading away

Pick the few tensions this work actually involves. Look for a visible tell, decide whether the finding concerns the whole direction or a local choice, and fix it there. Recheck the other side after the change.

| Tension | First failure | Other failure | A useful move |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Reading and computation | A well-set page cannot show its current state or answer an inquiry | Panels and metrics interrupt the argument | Keep the resting page readable. Let relations and tools appear when asked for. |
| Precision and atmosphere | Flat rules and empty ground could belong to any manual | Haze softens edges needed to read numbers | Keep text and values on stable ground. Put atmosphere in the material or figure. |
| Dense and breathable | Tiny type and touching groups hide the entry point | Long gaps cost travel and add no rhythm or relationship | Preserve meaningful groupings and give the eye deliberate pauses. |
| Structure and scaffolding | Edges drift and group membership is guessed | Rails and boxes appear before the content | Carry the underlying order with alignment, spacing, type and material. |
| Quiet and expressive | A home and a footnote look alike | Several regions demand attention at once | Give each page a baseline and spend the strongest treatment on its main idea. |
| Easy to use and surprising | Every part looks like the same template | Readers relearn ordinary controls just to reach content | Keep controls comprehensible and let material, scale or behavior carry the surprise. |
| Technical and human | Machine labels spread into reading and display | Precision disappears behind friendly phrasing | Keep addressing and data precise. Give reading prose a human rhythm. |
| Still and responsive | The page hides state that actually exists | Hover or loading displaces what the reader is reading | Give the resting document the facts and let inquiry reveal real state or relations. |
| Serious and curious | The object is credible and inert | A gimmick stands between the reader and the claim | Put curiosity in a relation a reader can discover and use. |
| Consistent and distinct | Different subjects receive the same treatment in new colors | Focus, keyboard and dismissal change on every crossing | Keep learned controls and change the identity through form, material, type and pacing. |

A popular aesthetic can be wrong for the work. A terminal costume and always-on glow can obscure an editorial claim. A visually quiet template can also strip the subject of character. Diagnose the role of the proposed change before fixing it by adding or removing decoration. A large space, a card or a returning effect can be right when its role survives comparison with the brief.

## Worked example

A scientific archive has a strong type hierarchy, but its figure panels and toolbars occupy the same plane. Moving all controls behind an unnamed icon would make discovery worse. Keep the open record, visible figure and ordinary destination links. Give optional comparison tools one named entry that opens beside the figure. Check the first view, the focused control and the result after dismissal. The archive can still read as a publication while showing that its comparison tools do real work.
