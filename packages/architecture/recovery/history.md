# Recover historical records and their references

Find the earlier record and what it actually linked to. Keep original records intact; report tested paths, versions, reader modes and unresolved recovery claims in the existing recovery review.

## Choose the check

- [Check recovered historical records](../historical-records.md#check).
- [Resolve historical bytes after a locator is reused](../historical-locations.md#check).
- [Verify recovered references from their original base](../historical-links.md#check).
- [Verify where restored metadata came from](../restored-metadata.md#check).

## Worked example

A report's old locator now returns a new version. Resolve its historical identity, read the saved bytes and reconstruct relative links from the original base. Compare linked material and authorship metadata with what the earlier report actually establishes.
