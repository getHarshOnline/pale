# Governance

Package ID: `pale.governance` · [Manifest](package.toml)

Find who can settle a question and carry out agreed work within its permissions. These are Pale’s policies; upstream cases test them without defining them.

A workspace selects entries from this family for the duties its people and agents perform. To read it directly, open the page for your task and follow its reading links.

## Policies and other resources

- [Do the agreed work and check its result](<execution.md>) · `execution` · `policy_family`
  Use the request's existing permissions, preserve other people's work, check the result and name any action still needed from its owner.
- [Find who can decide the question](<authority.md>) · `authority` · `policy_family`
  Find the source that can settle this question, distinguish decisions from observations, and hold only the action whose answer is missing.
