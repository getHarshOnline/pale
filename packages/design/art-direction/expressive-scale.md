# Set the expressive range and pace of a page

Give the ordinary reading state a visible character. Set a floor for the materials, type and held states that should be present at rest. Set a ceiling for where and how far expression may rise. The ceiling gives room to express an important event. A page need only rise as far as its subject calls for. Keep focus, reading and basic control behavior consistent as intensity changes. If a project has a named scale or a single-peak rule, follow its published design. Do not copy another project's mode names or thresholds into an unrelated work.

Distinguish the declared range from the observed page: record the required baseline, permitted peak, actual baseline and strongest rendered region with its location. A permitted high peak does not require spending all that permission. Falling below a declared floor is a different defect from exceeding the ceiling. Where the identity has an expressive envelope, read its actual adjustments at the owner; the envelope limits emphasis and does not replace the identity's material, type and behavior.

Sketch three to five beats such as `arrival → orientation → substance → consequence → exit`. Name what the reader should feel and understand at each one. If every section can swap places and keeps the same effect, the hierarchy has probably not reached the page. A strong opening still has to orient. Give the last screen a settled close or a next step within reach. Alternate dense and open passages by their content. Try space, type, and a material shift before reaching for lines, boxes or repeated bands.

Readers can arrive mid-task on a phone and leave between interruptions. Give them orientation and the main idea in the first view. Make each short passage useful on its own and place the peak where the handheld visit needs it. A wide comparison at a desk may become two well-matched stills in hand. In reduced motion, place the emphasis in the composed still, an immediate change of light or a material change. For dark presentation, adjust the light's strength and spread for the darker surroundings. With effects off, the reading ground, information and available controls should still feel complete.

Across a site, a visit can move from recognition to curiosity to discovery to fluency. Introduce advanced capability where it answers a real inquiry. Recognizable controls and reachable destinations support discovery. A home page that shows every apparatus at once gives away the rhythm. Review a chosen ambient field with its actual clock, contrast and pause behavior. If the strongest treatment can move to another page without loss, tie it to this page's meaning. If two expressive regions compete, first recover hierarchy through placement, timing or emphasis.

## Preserve a minimum presence and put the strongest moment where it earns attention

Set the floor for the identity at rest and the strongest expression the page can carry. The floor is the material, type, geometry and visible state a reader can still recognize without hovering. A floor at one level and a ceiling at another leave room for rest and a peak. If the source asks for greater presence, identify the missing treatment and restore it in a form that fits the task.

| Expression | What changes |
| --- | --- |
| At rest | Stable reading, recognizably different material and type, current state visible |
| Surface accent | Light or texture makes a real edge, group or surface legible |
| Contained expression | A figure, plate or substrate holds a deliberate contrast within the composition |
| Anchored departure | A significant region reaches beyond one normal edge while controls and reading stay placed |
| Immersive passage | A local change of field or authored sequence carries a central idea and has a way out |

Use the local project's permitted range. A reference or retrieval page may need the first two treatments. A narrative can spend more where it carries the subject. Orient the reader, develop the selected hierarchy of moments, then settle and give a useful next step. A rest is a composed frame with enough information and character to read. When a capability is absent, remove the expensive expressive behavior first and keep the information and action.

If a region competes with reading, first reduce its light, motion or framing while retaining meaningful state. Then adjust the surrounding baseline within its declared floor. Consider removing an element only after those changes, preserving content and access. In the other direction, recover missing presence within the existing ceiling before proposing a higher one. A reduced-motion or dark presentation needs an intentional carrier of the same emphasis; capability failure may need a simpler rendition that still communicates the information and held state. Judge a narrow recomposition from the result: less travel can preserve the peak, while a page that loses its main idea has not merely become smaller.

| Observation | Check and response |
| --- | --- |
| The main idea and every supporting region have equal intensity | Compare the intended beats with the rendered hierarchy; give the important idea its earned emphasis. |
| A minor control dominates while the central figure is ordinary | Move emphasis to the content without making navigation hard to find. |
| The reduced or dark view loses the important moment | Recompose its carrier through material, light, type or scale; a paused transition is not automatically a finished still. |
| Several regions compete | Inspect placement and timing against the selected attention pattern. Apply a numerical peak limit only where that pattern requires it. |
| The opening is resolved but the last screen is leftover links | Inspect first and last screens together; finish the useful close or reachable next step. |
| A narrow view delays the idea for many screens | Write the handheld visit's own beats and place the meaningful comparison where that visit needs it. |
| Home, index and reading pages look interchangeable | Compare their tasks and permitted ranges; preserve shared identity while composing the information differently. |

These page decisions govern the host. An embedded linear film follows its own authored rhythm and may have multiple peaks or a deliberate loop. Observe temporal pacing in motion; stills establish composition, not whether a reveal, hold or reversal works.

## Worked example

A lab report begins with a title, method and a readable scale. Its high point is a figure that exposes a limit of the method. The notes and download remain available throughout. A desk version lets the two samples sit beside one another. In hand, lead with the significant comparison and put its explanation and data link directly below. If the glowing navigation dominates the opening and the figure looks ordinary, lower the navigation's intensity and let the figure carry the contrast.
