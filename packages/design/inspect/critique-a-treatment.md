# Critique the treatment against the actual brief

Read the whole rendered page at its ordinary size and record the first, second and third things you see. Then read the original brief, corrections and selected references. Compare the observed order with the subject, orientation and action that should register. Follow [Judge the design through its working parts](lenses.md) for the verdict before using the builder’s explanation to diagnose it. A border, label or toolbar can be useful, and it can also pull attention away from the actual subject.

Stress the parts that matter. Try a long title, empty list, missing image, single record and repeated records where the page can reach those states. Press and focus the controls. Watch motion at normal speed, then slow it down and step through a troublesome turn or handoff. Inspect the first and last frames. For a scroll sequence, compare the same position approached from both directions. For a film, watch the connected cut and judge the hold in the intended player. Review again on the device that makes a different input or refresh behavior possible when the claim depends on it.

Describe the place, material, light and voice you actually see in one sentence. If the sentence needs the documentation to supply those qualities, the rendered work has not communicated them yet. If the same sentence fits a different subject without changing anything, identify what the reference should have contributed. Compare legible familiar controls separately from the subject’s visual character. A close reconstruction can rightly resemble its source.

## Tell what the repair should restore

| What you see | What to try |
| --- | --- |
| A section has a heavy frame, tinted strip or colored rail that only announces the section. | Remove it for a comparison. Use alignment, type and spacing to show the grouping. Keep a frame when the reader acts on a distinct object such as a form or figure. |
| A region can disappear and the composition stays the same. | Identify its job. A figure that explains something should make its relationship visible. Deliberate atmosphere can support the visit without making a claim. |
| A change quieted the wrong thing. | Compare before and after. If a complaint about a familiar display face removed the type character altogether, restore the intended axis with a better treatment. A new rule or sample board cannot show the correction landed on the page. |
| A prompt, timestamp or live light suggests a capability that cannot be exercised. | Bind it to actual behavior, remove the claim, or present it plainly as an illustration. A working indicator should show what it measures. |
| Haze softens edges or creates the same mood behind every passage. | Keep text and values on a stable surface. Give light an actual source, a state or a place in the subject. A narrative scene can make the haze itself the subject. |
| A staged figure or cinematic device recurs everywhere with no change of job. | Check which repetition conveys identity or comparison. Vary or remove the rest. Give the strongest moment to the actual claim or experience. |
| A control, generic template or demo palette supplies the apparent identity. | Recover the subject from the accepted references. Change the relevant material, type, light, geometry or rhythm. Keep learned interaction where it serves the reader. |

Look for a remedy in the rendered pages. If a strong approved treatment is visible in its specimen and absent in the delivered page, find the template, rule or placement that kept it from appearing. If a viewer needs to understand an unfolding action, watch it with the copy and without the explanatory text where that helps locate the fault.

## Try a quick alternative and diagnose its level

Read the opening, center and ending before looking at detector scores. Say what the page or film communicates in the brief's terms. A change to local polish is useful only if it addresses the failure. If the same fault appears across uses of one component, find its shared drawing. If the whole identity disappears except for its accent, examine material, shape, type and movement across several pages. If the primary claim is hard to find, compare a stripped view before adding a brighter label.

Check plan and output against the references' intended use. An original direction can avoid generic reuse. A reconstruction can intentionally match its reference and should be judged against what was requested. Remove one accessory at a time. A decorative grid, shadow, simulated meter, novelty cursor or elaborate heading may obscure the work. Keep an accessory whose removal loses a useful relationship, purpose or chosen expressive moment.

A finding names the location, input state, observation, reader consequence and likely owner of the correction. “Generic” becomes actionable when it says the four unrelated sections use the same card rhythm and the reader cannot distinguish the main claim from the supporting records. Show what the prior direction supplied and what the proposed fix changes. Keep the readiness judgment separate from the owner's decision.

## Worked example: move the finding to the right source

The brief calls for a restrained instrument inside a long article. The border around it is the heaviest shape on a blurred capture. Remove the border temporarily and read the actual values and their labels. If the relationship then reads clearly, reduce the repeated frame where the shared component defines it. If only this article’s placement is wrong, correct the placement. Name the screen and state that showed the problem, the observable result and the proposed correction. Reopen the page with the longest supported title after the repair.
